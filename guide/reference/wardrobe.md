# 👗 Wardrobe Catalogue

Tara's clothes are sewn, not bought off the rack. You buy a design from Violet, gather the cloth, leather, and thread it lists, and stitch it at a Sewing Machine in the tool shed. This page lists what every outfit takes, the total materials for sewing the whole rack, and the recipes for jewelry and hair.

## How sewing works {#sewing}

| Step | Where | What it takes |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Build a Sewing Machine | Tool shed, 1×2 space | 10 iron ingots, 5 silver ingots, 20 wood, 600 coins |
| 2. Buy a design | Violet's shop in the Town Center, open Tuesday to Saturday, 9am–5pm | 200 coins for an everyday design, 500 for a magic one |
| 3. Make the fabric | [Loom, dyeing bath, mortar & pestle, tanning station](../crafting/stations.md) | See the tables below |
| 4. Sew it | Sewing Machine | Instant |

- **Violet's Violets becomes Violet's Boutique** once she starts selling patterns, after a short introductory quest. Her flower seeds stay on sale in the same shop.
- **Selling outfits back to Violet stocks new designs.** Each outfit you sell raises her pattern level, so sewing cheap outfits and selling them is how you open up the rest of the rack.
- **Magic designs** unlock after a letter from Violet, which arrives once you have collected produce from a magical animal for the first time.
- **The Signature Wylde Jacket and Dress needs no design.** It is available as soon as the boutique opens and takes 1 red cloth, 1 black leather, and 1 plain leather (fish or mushroom leather also works for that last one).
- **Cloth** comes from the loom, using cotton, wool, or alpaca wool. **Silk** is woven from silk thread, which the silkworm box makes from mulberry leaves.
- **Leather** comes from the tanning station: tanning solution plus an animal hide, a fish, or a mushroom.
- **Colors** are dyes ground at the mortar & pestle, then applied to cloth, silk, leather, or thread in the dyeing bath.
- **Coatings** (glimmer and sparkle) are brewed in the cauldron. Shiny and rainbow cloth are woven from magical animals' wool.
- The changing room at the back of the boutique puts Tara in a random outfit.
