# 🏆 Achievements & Milestones

*Wylde Flowers* keeps **40 achievements** (on PlayStation: 32 bronze,
2 silver, 5 gold, and the platinum; a full 1,000 Gamerscore's worth on
Xbox-style lists; matching sets on Steam and elsewhere). The pleasant
news for completionists: almost everything unlocks by simply *playing
the whole game generously* — this is not a checklist of obscure
torments.

## The shape of the list

The achievements trace the arc of Tara's island life. A spoiler-light
sampling of confirmed milestones:

- **Meet all of the residents of Fairhaven** — introductions, done
  thoroughly (the [Character Directory](../characters/directory.html)
  helps).
- **Discover Gramma's basement** — the story provides; see
  [Chapter 2](../story/chapter-02.html) 🤫
- **Cast the initiation rite** — likewise;
  [Chapter 3](../story/chapter-03.html).
- **Adopt the cat** — mandatory in our hearts, achievement or no
  ([Familiars & Cat Form](../witchcraft/familiars.html)).
- **Restore the ranch** — the farm rebuilding arc, seen through.
- **Turn the season to Summer** — your first great rite
  ([Chapters 4–6](../story/chapters-04-06.html)).

…and onward through the story, the craft, and the island's systems.
The hidden entries are hidden because they're story spoilers — every
one of them arrives naturally by finishing the tale.

## The collection boards ⭐

Alongside achievements, Tara keeps roughly **twenty collection
boards** — fish, minerals, crops, dishes, and the island's other
catalogues — each completable for a **gold star**. The boards are the
real long game, and the fastest route through them is breadth:

- Fish **every water** with **every bait** (fish ignore season and
  time, mercifully — [Fishing](../farming/fishing.html)).
- Mine **every depth** — the deep floors hold the rare minerals
  ([Mining & Foraging](../farming/mining-and-foraging.html)).
- Grow **every crop in every season** — you control the calendar, so
  no harvest is ever truly missable
  ([Crop Guide](../farming/crops.html)).
- Cook **everything cookable** — the 160-plus recipe hunt is the
  longest board of all ([Cooking & Recipes](../crafting/cooking.html)).

## A completionist's counsel

1. **Finish the story first.** It unlocks regions, systems, and
   collection entries nothing else can.
2. **Sweep for stragglers after the ending** — post-game Fairhaven is
   the ideal era for board-filling, with all seasons freely yours.
3. **Recipes are the one place to stay alert** — a few dishes tie to
   particular moments; accept every recipe the game ever offers.
4. **The platinum is a farewell tour, not a grind.** By the time the
   list is done, you'll know every soul and stone on this island —
   which was always the real achievement.
