🧮 Crop Profit Calculator

Because Fairhaven's seasons only turn when you cast the ritual, the question is never "what month is it?" — it's "how long will I stay?" Tell the ledger your season and your stay, and it will rank every specimen by coins per day.

This calculator runs on the same field records as the Crop Guide, pressed from the community wiki (CC BY-SA 3.0, as is this ledger). Coins/day is the wiki's measure: (sell price of a full harvest − seed cost) ÷ days to grow — a full harvest is several items from one sowing, which is why a bed out-earns a single fruit.

How the ledger is kept

  • Profit per harvest is taken straight from the wiki's coins/day × days to grow — so a single sowing here always matches the Crop Guide's tables exactly.
  • Net profit multiplies that by how many harvests fit in your stay (replanting after each one when the box is ticked) and by your number of beds. Coins/day divides it by the days of your stay.
  • Cotton 🧵 can't be sold raw. Its ~39 coins/day — the best in the game — exists only at the loom, as cloth sold to Kai. Untick "I have a loom" and cotton politely leaves the table. See Artisan Goods.
  • Flowers 🌸 pay twice — if you don't pick them. A blooming flower left standing feeds your beehives; the coins/day above assumes you harvest and sell it instead. You can't have both from the same bloom.
  • Two specimens sit out for want of numbers: Hops can't be sold at all (it's for brewing), and the wiki ledger doesn't record Primrose's seed price — though community guides call it the most profitable flower in the game.
  • Crops that can't finish a single harvest inside your stay sink to the bottom of the table, marked as such. And harvest everything before you cast the ritual — out-of-season crops wilt the moment it lands (Farming 101).
