🧮 Crop Profit Calculator

Because Fairhaven's seasons only turn when you cast the ritual, the question is never "what month is it?" — it's "how long will I stay?" Tell the ledger your season and your stay, and it will rank every specimen by coins per day, raw or run through a crafting station.

Figures in this ledger are pressed from CozyProfit's crop database (which in turn draws on the community wiki). CozyProfit's baselines assume daily watering, no fertilizer, and midpoint estimates — so a few prices may differ from the Crop Guide's pressed almanac. Treat both as field notes, not gospel.

How the ledger is kept

  • Net profit is revenue minus seed cost across your whole stay, times your number of beds. Coins/day divides that by the days you stay.
  • Regrowing crops (berries, grapes, cocoa, fruit trees) pay for their seed once; every later harvest is pure profit. That's why long stays flatter them and short stays punish them.
  • Single-harvest crops are replanted after each harvest when the replant box is ticked — untick it to model a single sowing, which is CozyProfit's long-window baseline.
  • Best processed value is the most a single harvest can fetch after a crafting station has had its say — cotton at the loom, grapes as wine, and so on. It ignores station time and any extra ingredients, so treat it as a ceiling. See Artisan Goods for what transforms into what.
  • Crops that can't finish a single harvest inside your stay sink to the bottom of the table, marked as such.
  • Seasons are ritual-controlled, and out-of-season plants wilt the moment you cast it — harvest everything first (Farming 101).
