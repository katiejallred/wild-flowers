# 🍳 Cooking & Recipes

If the cauldron is the sanctum's heart, the **kitchen** is the farmhouse's —
and honestly, the kitchen might be the more powerful room. Food restores
Tara's energy, food earns coin, and food is the island's love language:
every friendship in Fairhaven runs on it.

## How cooking works

Cook at the kitchen in Gramma's farmhouse: pick a known recipe, supply the
ingredients, done. The craft is in the *pantry*, not the pan — keeping
crops, catches, forage, and animal products stocked so the recipe list
lights up when you need it.

## Where recipes come from

There are **over 160 dishes** to learn, and the game hides them
everywhere:

- **Default knowledge** — Tara arrives knowing the basics.
- **Quests** — story and side-quests regularly pay in recipes.
- **Recipe books at the shops** — check retailers' shelves as seasons and
  story progress.
- **Friendship** — villagers gift recipes as you grow close, often dishes
  from their own tables. (The fastest way to a recipe is through its
  owner's heart.)
- **The island itself** — smashing **big stones**, digging **dirt piles**,
  and **fishing the ocean** can all turn up recipes. Curiosity cooks.

A gentle collector's note: a few recipes are tied to particular moments and
easy to sail past, so when a quest or event offers food knowledge, take it
then and there.

## What cooking is *for*

1. **Energy.** Cooked meals restore far more than raw ingredients — a
   stocked lunchbox is farm infrastructure. (Coffee remains the famous
   coin-efficient standby.)
2. **Coin.** Cooking generally raises the value of ingredients; when the
   crop chest overflows, cook the surplus before selling.
3. **Gifts.** *Everyone in Fairhaven wants food*, and each person has
   particular favorite dishes worth several ordinary gifts. The known
   favorites are pressed into the
   [Gift Preferences](../characters/gift-preferences.html) page.
4. **The collection.** Yes, there's a board for dishes cooked. Of course
   there is. Fill it.

## Kitchen wisdom

- Cook with a purpose queue: today's energy meals, this week's gift dishes,
  and the surplus-for-sale batch.
- Before a season ritual, cook or store out-of-season produce — recipes
  don't stop wanting spring vegetables just because you summoned autumn.
- When you befriend someone new, learn what they love *first* and build the
  pantry backward from there.
