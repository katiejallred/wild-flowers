# 🌱 Beginner Tips

Gathered like seeds from many first playthroughs. None of these are required —
Fairhaven forgives everything — but each one smooths a bump we hit so you
don't have to.

## The essentials

1. **Quests first, always.** Every system — farming upgrades, the coop, the
   barn, the entire witchy half of the game — unlocks through the story. When
   in doubt, do the top item on the To-Do list.
2. **Empty the energy bar daily.** Leftover energy at bedtime is wasted. Turn
   it into wood, stone, and fish — the currencies of early-game progress.
3. **Coffee is your friend.** As energy-per-coin goes, it's famously the best
   value on the island. Keep a few cups on hand for big farm days.
4. **Expand the garden early.** More beds → more crops → more coin → more
   everything. Garden expansion pays for itself faster than any other
   purchase.

## Money & materials

5. **Stockpile wood and iron even when nothing needs them.** Something always
   will: the coop, the barn, the bridge, a crafting station, a boat that
   badly needs repairing. (You'll see.)
6. **Upgrade the pickaxe as soon as copper allows.** A better pickaxe makes
   the mine dramatically faster, and the mine funds the rest of your life.
   See [Mining & Foraging](../farming/mining-and-foraging.html).
7. **Cook before you sell.** A cooked dish is generally worth more than its
   ingredients — and doubles as a gift. See
   [Cooking & Recipes](../crafting/cooking.html).

## Around the island

8. **Smash big stones and dig up dirt piles.** They sometimes hide recipes
   and other treats. So does fishing in the ocean. Curiosity is rewarded here.
9. **Check every shop's board.** Weekly hours are posted outside each shop —
   a ten-second read that saves many wasted walks.
10. **Give people their favorite foods.** Everyone on this island loves to
    eat, and a favorite dish earns far more friendship than a generic gift.
    Start with the verified favorites on the
    [Gift Preferences](../characters/gift-preferences.html) page.

## The one warning this game actually needs

11. **Don't perform a season-changing ritual with crops in the ground.**
    Out-of-season plants wilt the moment the season turns. Harvest first,
    *then* ritual. The game asks you to confirm — that pause is for you.

12. **Pet the animals every day.** For the friendship bonus, yes. But mostly
    because look at them.
