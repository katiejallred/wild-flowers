# 🔮 Spells & Rituals

Spellcraft in Fairhaven is a paperwork job, in the most charming way:
incantations are **crafted at the desk** in your sanctum — ink, paper, and a
few precious reagents — then carried in your pocket and *recited* where
they're needed.

## Learning spells

Spells arrive through coven quests. The Farseer's *Incantation Vexation*
questline is your formal introduction to the desk, and from there each rank
of trust unlocks stranger and better pages for your grimoire. You can't rush
it, and you won't want to — every spell comes wrapped in story.

## A sampling of the grimoire

A few known incantations, to give the flavor of the thing:

| Incantation | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Summon Wind** | Calls up a day of wind |
| **Summon Storm** | Brings strong winds and lightning — good for one storm |
| **Lockpick** | Opens what is closed (the ethics are between you and the coven) |
| **Freeze Time** | Stops the clock, briefly — the busy witch's best friend |
| **Reverse Time** | Winds the day's hours backward |
| **Animal Shapeshifting** | The famous one. See [Familiars & Cat Form](familiars.html) 🐈‍⬛ |
| **Fealty to the Fair Folk** | Sworn to open the way into the fair folk's world (1 black ink, 1 paper, 3 amethyst) |
| **Seasonal Ritual Incantations** | One per season — see below |

Reagent lists live on the desk itself when you craft; expect inks, papers,
powders, and gemstones — which is why the [mine](../farming/mining-and-foraging.html)
never stops mattering.

## The great rites: changing the season

The island's showpiece magic. Each season has its own **Ritual
Incantation** — spring, summer, fall, winter — performed **at the coven's
stone circle**, with the coven in attendance and the High Priestess
presiding.

The first turning is a story quest (*To Summer It Up For You*), and it
teaches the recipe's shape: beyond the incantation itself you'll brew a
**Spirit Flame**, which wants a bit of supply-chain witchery —

- **Essence of Copper** — 2 copper ore, run through the distillery
- **Powdered Calcium** — 2 seashells, ground at the mortar and pestle

…which is to say: the beach and the mine are ritual suppliers. Stock both.

**The one warning, in bold, again:** performing a seasonal rite **wilts every
out-of-season crop instantly**. Harvest first. The High Priestess will ask if
you're truly ready — the correct answer, the first time, is "one moment,"
followed by a sprint to the turnip beds.

## Broom flight

Yes — in time, the coven will see you airborne. Fairhaven from a broom at
night is one of the game's quiet rewards, and that's all this journal will
say about it.
