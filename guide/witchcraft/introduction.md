# 🔮 The Craft: An Introduction

*Mild early-game spoilers: this page assumes you've learned what Gramma does
after dark. If you haven't, go play the first days — it's better felt than
read.*

Fairhaven has two lives. By day it's a farming town; by night, a **coven of
witches** in robes and masks keeps the island's deeper machinery — its
seasons, its weather, its secrets — turning. Hazel is one of them. And Tara,
it turns out, has the family gift.

## Becoming a witch

You don't get handed a wand. Joining the coven is a story: introductions in
the moonlit grove, tasks to prove your sincerity, and finally the
**initiation rite** — which, in fine Fairhaven tradition, you must craft and
cast yourself. As the High Priestess puts it: you're not a witch *yet*.

From there, magic unlocks the way everything in this game does — through
quests. Each coven task teaches a new corner of the craft, and Tara's
abilities grow in step with her standing among the masked.

## The sanctum

Beneath the farmhouse lies the **basement** — Gramma's sanctum, and soon
yours. It begins humbly outfitted and grows into a full magical workshop as
you add stations:

| Station | Craft |
|---|---|
| **Cauldron** | Potions — the sanctum's founding appliance |
| **Desk** | Incantations (spells on paper), and the ink to write them |
| **Distillery** | Essences and spirits — ingredients for potions and stronger drink |
| **Mortar & Pestle** | Powders and dyes, ground fine |
| **Crystal Ball** | Hints and glimpses — including your animals' secret favorite foods |
| **Scrying Bowl** | Farther seeing still |

The pattern mirrors the tool shed upstairs: raw materials in, refined magic
out. A great deal of witchcraft, it turns out, is *logistics* — ore becomes
essence becomes potion becomes a changed season. Plan your crafting chains
like the practical witch you are.

## The shape of the craft

Four broad disciplines, each with its own page in this journal:

- **[Potions](potions.html)** — brewed in the cauldron, from grow-juice to
  rarer wonders.
- **[Spells & rituals](spells-and-rituals.html)** — incantations from the
  desk, and the great seasonal rites at the stone circle.
- **[Weather magic](weather-magic.html)** — wind, storm, and the island's
  quirky skies.
- **[Shapes & companions](familiars.html)** — the cat business. You'll love
  the cat business.

And running under all of it: the **Wellspring**, the source of the island's
magic, which the coven tends and which the story will teach you to care
about very much indeed. But that belongs to the
[story pages](../story/overview.html), and this journal keeps its spoilers
pressed in clearly marked drawers.
