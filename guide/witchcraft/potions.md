# 🧪 Potion Brewing

The **cauldron** is the sanctum's founding appliance — it's there when you
first descend the basement stairs, patient as a kettle. Everything else in
the room exists, more or less, to feed it.

## How brewing works

Potions are recipes: gather the listed ingredients, visit the cauldron, brew.
No minigame, no chance of a face full of soot — the challenge is entirely in
the **supply chain**, because potion ingredients are often *themselves
crafted*:

- The **distillery** turns raw materials into **essences** (ore into Essence
  of Copper, and so on).
- The **mortar and pestle** grinds shells, minerals, and botanicals into
  **powders**.
- The farm, the mine, the beach, and the forest supply the raw goods
  beneath it all.

A witch's shopping list, read backwards, is a map of the whole island.

## Learning recipes

Potion recipes come from **coven quests**, each rank of trust unlocking more
of the book. An early example of the pattern: the coven's *Something's
Brewing* task asks you to brew a batch of **Miracle Grow Potion** — and
graduating from it earns you the **Revitalizing Potion** recipe. Brew what
they ask, and the book fattens.

## The working potions

The everyday brews earn their shelf space fast:

- **Miracle Grow** — the farmer-witch's crossover hit; magic in service of
  the vegetable beds.
- **Revitalizing Potion** — restoration in a bottle.
- **Spirit Flame** — the ritual component that powers the great seasonal
  rites; see [Spells & Rituals](spells-and-rituals.html).

And beyond the everyday, the story will ask the cauldron for stranger
things — potions tied to particular people and particular secrets that this
page refuses to spoil. When a quest hands you an odd recipe, know that it's
never busywork: in Fairhaven, every brew is plot.

## Brewing tips

1. **Keep base stock on hand:** ores for essences, shells for powders, herbs
   and flowers from the farm. Ingredients keep; gathering trips at the
   moment of need are how bedtimes get missed.
2. **Brew extras.** If a quest wants three of something, the recipe is worth
   knowing — and a spare potion in the chest has a way of becoming useful.
3. **Read the cauldron's list before the mine trip,** not after. The
   distillery cannot distill ore you didn't bring home.
