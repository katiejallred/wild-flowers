# 🌦️ Weather Magic

Every farmer scans the sky. A witch-farmer *edits* it. Weather-working is where the two halves of Tara's life shake hands most profitably: the spells live in your grimoire, but the payoff lands squarely in your fields and fishing spots.

## The Weather Spells

Weather magic arrives, like all magic here, through the main story and coven tasks (see [Spells & Rituals](spells-and-rituals.md) for how incantations are learned, written at the desk, and consumed when read aloud). The verified weather workings:

| Incantation | Effect | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Summon Rain Spell | Calls a day of rain | One day |
| Summon Wind Spell | Sets the wind blowing | One day |
| Summon Storm Spell | Strong winds and lightning | One storm |
| Summon Night Spell | Not weather, strictly — but it brings the dark, and pairs beautifully with a stormy mood | Until morning |

Remember the golden rule of incantations: each written scroll is **consumed on casting**. A day of rain costs you the magic and ingredients that went into writing it — worth every drop at the right moment, wasteful on a week you'd have watered anyway.

## Rain: The Farmer's Cheat Code

The **Summon Rain Spell** is the one you'll write again and again. Rain waters every planted tile at once — no watering can, no trudging, no energy spent — which converts directly into hours of your morning back. Point that windfall at the [mines](../farming/mining-and-foraging.md), the [fishing rod](../farming/fishing.md), or your long-suffering social calendar ([Friendship](../characters/friendship.md)).

### When rain magic earns its keep

- **Big planting days.** Sowed half the farm this morning? Summon rain tonight and skip tomorrow's watering marathon entirely.
- **Summer droughts.** Fairhaven's summers run hot and dry, and thirsty crops and trees drain the island's groundwater — there's even a story stretch (*For Crying Drought Loud*) where the drought becomes everyone's problem and rain magic becomes the answer. When wells run low, the mundane fallback is bottled water from the Dahl General Store at 60 coins a bottle — or free from Cameron on Wednesdays and Sundays, bless his suspicious heart. The magical fallback is better.
- **Energy crunches.** A rainy day plus a [Revitalizing Potion](potions.md) is functionally a two-for-one day.

> Lore aside: for generations, Fairhaven's "lucky weather" has been the coven quietly doing its chores. Cameron's League of the Conscious Mind thanks providence; providence wears a mask and a pointy hat.

## Wind & Storm

The blowier siblings get less daily use but have their moments:

- **Summon Wind Spell** — a day of moving air. Story tasks and certain workings want weather with a bit of drama; wind is the gentle option.
- **Summon Storm Spell** — one full storm, winds and lightning included. Storms bring rain's crop-watering benefits wrapped in considerably more atmosphere, and some quests and moods simply *call* for a storm. Cast it when the plot (or your inner theater kid) demands.

### Weather and the wider game

| Activity | How summoned weather touches it |
| --- | --- |
| Farming | Rain and storms water crops for free; drought pressure eases |
| Fishing | Weather shifts the mood at the water — anglers chasing variety should experiment on summoned days ([Fishing](../farming/fishing.md)) |
| Foraging & town life | Rainy days feel different in Fairhaven — townsfolk and routines carry on, but your umbrella-free witch stays busy indoors or in the mines |
| Rituals & quests | Some story moments expect particular skies; the quest log will tell you when to reach for the storm scroll |

## A Weather-Witch's Planning Table

| Situation | Reach for |
| --- | --- |
| 40 tiles freshly planted | Summon Rain, cast at night |
| Drought event mid-summer | Summon Rain (and pocket Cameron's free water anyway) |
| Quest wants dramatic weather | Summon Storm |
| Just need the day to *end* | Summon Night, then bed |
| Season about to turn | No weather spell saves out-of-season crops — harvest, then ritual ([Spells & Rituals](spells-and-rituals.md)) |

## Fine Print for New Weather-Workers

1. Weather spells last about a day — this is a nudge to the sky, not a new climate.
2. Scrolls are single-use; keep one spare rain scroll written for emergencies.
3. Weather magic is unlocked partway into the witch story — early-game farmers still befriend their watering can. See [Farming 101](../farming/farming-101.md).
4. Don't summon rain out of habit; summon it out of arithmetic. If the watering would cost more energy than the scroll cost to write, cast away.
