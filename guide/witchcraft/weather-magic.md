# 🌦️ Weather Magic

Fairhaven's weather is *managed*. Not metaphorically — the coven tends the
island's skies and seasons as a duty, the way other towns tend roads. This
is, incidentally, why the town's resident **meteorologist, Giva**, is here at
all: she came to study why the local weather is so magnificently quirky.
Science deserves answers. Science is not going to get them from *us*.

## The weather spells

Two workhorse incantations, crafted at the desk like any other
(see [Spells & Rituals](spells-and-rituals.html)):

| Incantation | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Summon Wind** | Sets the wind blowing for a day |
| **Summon Storm** | Strong winds and lightning — good for one storm |

Why command the weather? Because certain things on the island only happen
under certain skies. When a quest, a crop, or a curiosity wants wind or
storm, the spell means never waiting on luck. (And a summoned storm doing
your watering has a satisfaction all its own.)

## The big lever: seasons

The grandest weather magic isn't a spell but a **rite**. The four Seasonal
Ritual Incantations — performed at the stone circle with the full coven —
are how the island moves from spring to summer to fall to winter at all.
Until Tara can perform them, the calendar simply *waits*.

The rites get their own full write-up in
[Spells & Rituals](spells-and-rituals.html); the short version:

- Each rite needs its incantation plus brewed components (the **Spirit
  Flame** among them).
- The change is instant and total — including the instant wilting of every
  out-of-season crop. **Harvest first.**
- After the main story, the wheel turns freely, and the island's weather
  becomes, fully and officially, your hobby.

## Etiquette for sky-keepers

1. Check the fields before any weather work — storms and seasons don't ask
   the turnips' permission.
2. If a quest is waiting on particular weather, summon it rather than
   sleeping through days hoping.
3. Be gracious when Giva shows you her charts. Nod. Say "fascinating."
   *Mean it.* She's closer than she knows.
