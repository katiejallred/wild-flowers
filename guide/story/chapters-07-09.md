# 📜 Chapters 7–9

<p class="handwritten">spoiler level: 🌒 late-game — new realms and a gathering threat. the finale itself stays sealed until the next page.</p>

Fairhaven's map has been holding out on you. These chapters open its
hidden geography — and let something worrying in through the gap.

## Chapter 7: The Gloaming

Advancement in the coven brings Tara to the **Gloaming** — the twilight
realm that lies alongside the island, known only to witches. It is
beautiful, strange, and *useful*: the Gloaming keeps its own fish (the
collection board has been waiting), its own gathering grounds, and its
own rules. It is also, as the coven teaches, connected to everything
the witches guard — the **Wellspring** above all. Walk respectfully.

## Chapter 8: Steeped in Mystery

After the Summer Festival, the **Farseer** brings a quest with a
personal flavor: *Steeped in Mystery*, the road to the fair folk.

1. **Find the dilapidated boat** at the lake by your farm — yes, that
   sad old hull you've walked past all year.
2. **Repair it**: 15 wood, 5 iron ingots, 2 silver ingots. (The
   stockpiling habit pays its dividend at last.)
3. **Craft the Fealty to the Fair Folk spell** at the desk — 1 black
   ink, 1 paper, 3 amethyst — and swear yourself to the old courtesies.

The reward is a new horizon: the boat now ferries Tara — instantly,
whenever she likes — between her own lake and **Ravenwood Hollow**,
home of the **fair folk**. A whole second community opens: new faces,
new friendships, new shelves to browse (the Hollow's folklore-loving
bookseller, **Westley**, being of particular interest to a certain
kind of heart — see the [Romance Guide](../characters/romance.html)).

## Chapter 9: The Stranger at the Grove

Threaded through these discoveries, the story's shadow lengthens. A
masked figure calling themselves **Raven** — a witch, and no friend of
the coven's — has been circling Fairhaven's secrets, working to gain
entry to **the grove** where the coven meets. The witches close ranks;
suspicions are traded; and newcomers to the island start drawing
second glances. Who is Raven? What do they want with the Wellspring?

The coven's autumn is watchful. Yours should be too: keep up with
every coven quest, keep your craft sharp, and keep an eye on anyone
the story lingers on. When the masks finally come off — Raven's
included — everything changes.

The answers, all of them, wait in the
[Final Chapters](final-chapters.html). Seal your teacup and turn the
page only when ready.
