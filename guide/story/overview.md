# 📜 Chapter Guide Overview

<p class="handwritten">⚠️ story pages carry spoilers — each page warns you of how deep it goes. tread as suits you.</p>

*Wylde Flowers* tells a complete story — a beginning, a middle, and a
genuine ending — through its quest lines. The game itself doesn't number
chapters; it unfolds through your **To-Do list**, season by season. This
journal presses that flow into chapters for convenient reading, following
the natural arc of the seasons themselves.

## The shape of the story

**The first spring** — Tara arrives to help her ailing grandmother, learns
the farm… and learns what Hazel actually is. The family secret, the coven
in the grove, and Tara's own initiation all bloom in spring — as does the
spring's heartbreak. *(Chapters [1](chapter-01.html) ·
[2](chapter-02.html) · [3](chapter-03.html))*

**Summer and the unmasking** — Tara takes up her inheritance, turns her
first season, celebrates the Summer Festival, and begins the long,
delicious work of discovering who is behind each of the coven's five
masks. *(Chapters [4–6](chapters-04-06.html))*

**Deeper waters** — the mine's depths, the twilight realm of the Gloaming,
a boat rebuilt on the farm lake, the fair folk of Ravenwood Hollow — and,
threading through it all, a masked stranger called **Raven** with designs
on the coven's grove. *(Chapters [7–9](chapters-07-09.html))*

**The reckoning** — who Raven is, what the Wellspring truly holds, and
what it costs to keep an island's magic alive.
*(The [Final Chapters](final-chapters.html))*

Around and between: [side quests](side-quests.html), festivals, romances,
and two years of island life.

## How to use these pages

- **Spoiler ladder:** Chapter 1 is nearly spoiler-free; each page after
  descends one rung. The Final Chapters page spoils *everything*, and
  says so at the door.
- **Stuck?** The game's To-Do list nearly always contains your answer —
  our pages add context, warnings (harvest before rituals!), and the
  occasional handkerchief advisory.
- **Playing spoiler-free?** The honest counsel: this story is very good,
  and quests never let you get truly lost. Consider walking it blind and
  keeping this journal for the systems pages. We'll still be here after.

## A note on pacing

Nothing in the main story is missable through slowness. Seasons wait for
your ritual; quests wait for your readiness. The one thing worth knowing
in advance — spring's end brings the story's first great grief, and the
game handles it with more tenderness than any farm sim has a right to.
Bring tea.
