# 🏪 Shops & Services

Fairhaven's economy fits in a town square and spills charmingly over its
edges. Every business posts its **weekly hours on a board outside the
door** — check the board, plan the errand run, thank us later.

## Buying & selling essentials

- **Dahl General Store** — *Lina Dahl-Johnson.* The hub: seeds, produce,
  and sundries for sale, and the main counter where Tara **sells her
  crops**. If you visit one shop a day, it's this one.
- **Kim's Local Butcher and Deli** — *Kim Izumi.* Meats and deli goods;
  also a buyer of farm animals when it's time to part ways.
- **Soft and Son's Fish & Tackle** — *Bruno Soft*, by the statues. Rods,
  bait, and fishing wisdom, dispensed with paternal warmth.
- **Seton's Bakery** — *Angus Seton.* Desserts sold — and Angus will
  happily **buy your baking** too. Next door to his fiancé's shop, by
  careful romantic town-planning.
- **The jewelry shop** — *Francis.* Sparkle for sale; a natural home for
  the gems your pickaxe liberates.
- **Cafe Moretti** — *the Moretti family.* Coffee and comfort. (Coffee,
  the energy economist's beverage of choice.)
- **The bar** — *Damon Mthembu-Haas.* Fairhaven's only nightlife, and it's
  enough.

## Trades & services

- **Nailed It!** — *Parker Johnson*, carpentry, on the town's outskirts.
  Your partner in every farm building project.
- **The blacksmith** — *Natalia Kuznetsova.* Tools and toolwork; the
  pickaxe-upgrade pilgrimage starts here.
- **Marty's livestock** — *Marty.* Calves, lambs, and hatching eggs; also
  buys animals back. The barn's whole supply chain, one dealer.
- **Eury's Salon** — *Eury Sinclair.* Fairhaven's newest storefront: a
  proper hairdresser with a dazzling range of styles for Tara. Go for the
  cut, stay for the conversation.
- **Dr. Syed's General Practice** — *Amira Syed.* The town's doctor,
  reassuringly overqualified for splinters.
- **The flower shop** — *Violet Miranda*, beside the Town Hall. Blooms
  for gifts, gardens, and bees.

## Off the square

- **Kai's Treasures** — *Kai Hoapili's* novelty stall in the town square;
  treasures is the honest word for the inventory's ambitions.
- **Shelby's Scraps and Sundries** — *Shelby Demeter*, in the woods. Buys
  and sells beach, forest, and mountain finds — the forager's fence, er,
  *partner*.

## Merchant wisdom

Match the goods to the buyer: crops to Lina, baking to Angus, gems to
Francis, forage to Shelby, retired livestock to Kim or Marty. The right
counter is the difference between selling and *selling well* — details in
the [Money-Making Guide](../reference/money-making.html).
