# 🏪 Shops & Services

A field botanist is only as good as her suppliers. Fairhaven's shopkeepers are also your neighbors — and future friends — so every coin spent doubles as a social call. Shops keep regular business hours and close for the night, so plan your errands for daytime; exact hours vary by shop, and festival days can shuffle everyone's schedule. (Every storefront below is pinned on the [Interactive Island Map](fishing-map.html), if you'd rather shop by sight.)

---

## 🌱 Dahl General Store

**Proprietor:** Lina Dahl-Johnson (with her husband Parker Johnson helping run the place)

The beating heart of your farming operation, right in the town square. Lina stocks **seeds** for the current season, produce, and other food items — and the store is also the main place to **sell your crops**. Lina herself is the coven's High Priestess, so your friendly neighborhood grocer moonlights as your magical superior. Seed stock rotates with the season, so restock after every [season ritual](../witchcraft/spells-and-rituals.md).

<span class="handwritten">buy next season's seeds the morning after the ritual — beat the rush ❀</span>

## 🥩 Kim's Local Butcher and Deli

**Proprietor:** Kim Izumi (they/them)

Kim buys and sells meat products, and will also **buy adult animals** from your farm in exchange for coins and resources — the practical end of [animal husbandry](../farming/animals.md), handled with Kim's characteristic warmth. A good stop for cooking ingredients you can't raise yourself. (Kim is also one of the island's [romance options](../characters/romance.md).)

## 🍝 Cafe Moretti

**Proprietor:** Sophia Moretti — this is the beloved "diner" you'll see mentioned throughout this guide

Sophia's diner serves hearty cooked meals — useful when your energy is flagging and your own [kitchen](../crafting/cooking.md) is bare. It's also one of the town's great gathering spots; you'll overhear plenty of island life over a plate here.

## ⚒️ The Blacksmith

**Proprietor:** Natalia Kuznetsova

On the west side of town, next door to your farm. Natalia **smelts ores into ingots** and sells **upgraded farm equipment** — the essential stop for turning your [mining hauls](../farming/mining-and-foraging.md) into better tools. Tool upgrades are among the best investments in the game: they stretch your energy and your patience alike.

## 🏺 Kai's Treasures

**Proprietor:** Kai Hoapili

Kai, the island's friendly traveling merchant, keeps a shop of curiosities and wares in town near the waterfront. His stock leans toward the interesting rather than the everyday — worth a browse whenever you pass. (Also a [romance option](../characters/romance.md), and a charming one.)

## 🩺 Dr. Syed's General Practice

**Proprietor:** Dr. Amira Syed

The island's clinic. Amira is both the town doctor and a member of the coven — science and magic in one steady pair of hands. (Yes, [romanceable](../characters/romance.md) too.)

## 💇 Coiffed & Crowned by Eury

**Proprietor:** Eury Sinclair — *opens in Year 2*

When Eury arrives in Year 2, they open a hair salon where you can finally change Tara's look. A welcome bit of self-care after a year of farming — and Eury, naturally, joins the roster of [romance options](../characters/romance.md).

## 🧺 Selling Your Goods

- **Crops** sell at the Dahl General Store; **animals and meat** through Kim's; **fish** at Soft and Son's Fish & Tackle; **cooked dishes** at Cafe Moretti; **ores, ingots, and gems** at the Blacksmith. Full table in the [Money-Making Guide](../reference/money-making.md).
- Processed [artisan goods](../crafting/artisan-goods.md) generally out-earn raw ingredients — see the [Money-Making Guide](../reference/money-making.md) before you sell a raw harvest.
- Neighbors' to-do quests often pay in coins *and* goodwill; check them before dumping surplus at the store.

<span class="handwritten">sign-off tip: befriend your suppliers — gifts are cheap, and shopkeeper friendships pay off in story and smiles ❀</span>
