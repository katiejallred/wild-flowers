The fishing notes hold the theory; this plate is the field chart. Hover over (or tap) any marked water on the map below and the compendium opens to that page — every fish that bites there, the bait that tempts it, and what Bruno pays. Remember Fairhaven's great mercy: only place and bait matter. No seasons, no weather, no clocks.
