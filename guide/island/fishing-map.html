🗺️ Interactive Fishing & Foraging Map

The fishing notes and the forager's pages hold the theory; this plate is the field chart. Choose your errand below — rod or basket — then hover over (or tap) any marked spot and the compendium opens to that page. Two different laws govern the two pursuits: fish care only for place and bait (no seasons, no weather, no clocks), while forage cares for place and season — the wheel must turn before some patches will give.
