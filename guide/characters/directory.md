# 💛 Character Directory

Every field guide needs a census. Fairhaven is a small island with a large heart — roughly thirty residents, each with their own routines, opinions, and (whisper it) relationship values you can nurture like seedlings. Below, your pressed-and-labelled catalogue of everyone you'll meet, grouped by where they tend to take root.

Talk to everyone, every day, the way you'd water every pot on the sill. For raising those bonds deliberately, see the [Friendship Guide](friendship.md); for courting, the [Romance Guide](romance.md); and for what to tuck into your basket before knocking on doors, [Gift Preferences](gift-preferences.md).

> Residents marked ♥ are romanceable — all eight of them. Fairhaven's dating pool has one quiet rule: every candidate is single, with no relatives on the island.
