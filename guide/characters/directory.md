# 💛 Character Directory

Around **thirty souls** call Fairhaven home, every one of them fully
voice-acted and worth knowing. This directory keeps to daylight facts —
who runs what, who loves whom — and leaves the island's *other* rosters to
the [story pages](../story/overview.html), where spoilers are properly
pressed. 🤫

## The Wylde family

- **Tara Wylde** — that's you: city expat, new farmer, and rather more,
  in time.
- **Hazel Wylde** — Tara's grandmother, pillar of the community, keeper of
  the farm and of a certain family tradition.

## Town hall & public life

- **Otto Soft** — Mayor of Fairhaven; sends the letters that start half
  your festivals.
- **Antonio Miranda** — the mayor *before* Otto; the Miranda name still
  carries weight.

## Shopkeepers & tradespeople

- **Lina Dahl-Johnson** — runs **Dahl General Store**, the town's hub for
  seeds and produce; married to Parker.
- **Parker Johnson** — carpenter at **Nailed It!** on the town's edge;
  your man for building projects.
- **Kim Izumi** (they/them) — the butcher, at **Kim's Local Butcher and
  Deli**.
- **Angus Seton** — the baker at **Seton's Bakery**; engaged to Francis,
  whose shop is conveniently next door.
- **Francis** — the jeweler; buys gems, sells sparkle, adores Angus.
- **Natalia Kuznetsova** — the blacksmith; see her about tools.
- **Damon Mthembu-Haas** — keeps Fairhaven's only bar; came from
  Johannesburg and stayed.
- **Amira Syed** — the town doctor, at **Dr. Syed's General Practice**.
- **Bruno Soft** — fisherman, of **Soft and Son's Fish & Tackle**; hands
  out first rods with paternal pride. **Gloria** is family to the Softs —
  and that's all this page will say.
- **Sophia Moretti** — of **Cafe Moretti**; her daughter **Juliet** is
  often nearby.
- **Kai Hoapili** — proprietor of the novelty stall **Kai's Treasures**
  in the town square.
- **Eury Sinclair** — hair stylist from New Orleans, of **Eury's Salon**;
  Fairhaven's newest storefront.
- **Marty** — the livestock dealer; every calf, lamb, and chick on your
  farm came through Marty.
- **Shelby Demeter** — a marvelous old fellow in the woods, of **Shelby's
  Scraps and Sundries**, dealing in beach, forest, and mountain finds.

## Neighbors & notables

- **Violet Miranda** — of the flower shop by the Town Hall; Antonio's
  family, with young **Sebastian** as her little brother.
- **Cameron Conner** — once a Hollywood child actor, now the earnest local
  face of the **League of the Conscious Mind**. Fairhaven contains
  multitudes.
- **Giva Joshi** — meteorologist, on the island to study its famously
  quirky weather. We wish her luck. So much luck.
- **Westley Vuk** — a bookseller with a passion for folklore, met when the
  map grows beyond Fairhaven's shores. Filed here without further comment,
  spoilers being what they are. 🌙
- **Vanessa** — a newcomer whose story belongs entirely to the
  [story pages](../story/overview.html).

## Using this directory

Each resident keeps their own schedule — shops post weekly hours on boards
outside — and each has favorite foods that fast-track friendship (see
[Gift Preferences](gift-preferences.html)). Eight of the hearts above are
open to romance; the [Romance Guide](romance.html) names names.
