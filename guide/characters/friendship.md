# 💛 Friendship Guide

Fairhaven's real progression system isn't the farm — it's the neighbors.
Nearly everything warm in this game (recipes, story scenes, romance, the
occasional life-changing secret) flows through friendship, and friendship
flows through three simple habits.

## The three habits

1. **Talk to people.** Daily conversation builds bonds, and with a fully
   voice-acted cast, it's a pleasure rather than a chore. Dialogue shifts
   with the story, the season, and your standing — people *notice* your
   life here.
2. **Give food.** The island's love language. Everyone appreciates a meal,
   and every person has a short list of **favorite dishes** worth far more
   friendship than a generic bite. Learn a favorite, cook a batch, watch a
   relationship bloom. The verified lists live on the
   [Gift Preferences](gift-preferences.html) page.
3. **Do favors.** The bulletin board by the Town Hall and personal
   requests both deepen ties — and quests done for a person are worth more
   than any casserole.

## What friendship unlocks

- **Recipes** — friends share dishes from their own tables as you grow
  close; some recipes come no other way.
- **Story scenes** — closer friends open up: histories, troubles, small
  confessions. Some of the game's best writing hides at high hearts.
- **Romance** — courtship is friendship continued with intent; see the
  [Romance Guide](romance.html).
- **Milestones** — meeting everyone, and befriending the island broadly,
  both earn recognition (see
  [Achievements](../reference/achievements.html)).

## Practical matchmaking (the platonic kind)

- **Batch-cook favorites.** When a dish is one person's favorite, cook
  several — friendship is a repeat customer.
- **Follow the schedules.** Shop boards tell you where merchants are;
  everyone else keeps habits you'll learn by living here. When you can't
  find someone, try their workplace during business hours.
- **Mind the story.** Major quests introduce and deepen characters —
  a neighbor who seems closed off may simply be waiting for the plot to
  reach them.
- **Don't optimize the soul out of it.** One genuine dish a day beats a
  wheelbarrow of spam gifts, in spirit if not in arithmetic. This game was
  built to be *felt*.
