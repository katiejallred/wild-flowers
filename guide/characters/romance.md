# 💛 Romance Guide

Some friendships, given enough sun and patience, flower into something rarer. Eight of Fairhaven's residents can be courted, partnered, and — should the season be right — married. This page is your pollination chart: who's eligible, how affection grows, and what commitment looks like on a small island where everyone knows everyone.

New to tending relationships at all? Root yourself in the [Friendship Guide](friendship.md) first — romance grows from the same soil.

### Who *isn't* eligible — and why

Notice the pattern in the table above: every candidate is **single, with no relatives living on the island**. That's the game's quiet rule of thumb. It's why Angus and Francis (happily coupled), Lina and Parker (married, with twins), and Violet Miranda — the flower shop's owner, who shares a surname with another islander — tend their own gardens without you. If someone has family roots in Fairhaven or a partner already, they're flora to admire, not to pick.

## Inclusive by Design

All eight options are available to every player, **regardless of Tara's gender presentation or your choices** — there are no locked paths and no penalties. Fairhaven's romances span genders and identities as a matter of course (Kim is non-binary, and the island doesn't blink), and the wider cast includes married couples of all kinds, Angus and Francis among them. Love here is treated like weather: it simply happens, and everyone carries on.

## How Courtship Grows

### Hearts and cutscenes

Each candidate has an affection meter measured in **hearts, up to six**. Raise it the usual ways — daily talk, well-chosen gifts (see [Gift Preferences](gift-preferences.md)), and their personal quests. Each new heart you fill unlocks a **new cutscene**, advancing your relationship to its next stage. The stages, in bloom order:

1. **First date** — once affection is high enough, the candidate offers a quest that serves as your first date and formally opens the romance path.
2. **Dating** — after the first date, you may continue seeing them (or gently decline).
3. **Partnership** — keep filling hearts and the relationship deepens into a committed partnership.
4. **Engagement** — a ring exchange seals the promise.
5. **Marriage** — with the ring handled and affection at six hearts, a final cutscene arrives in which you and your beloved agree to marry.

### Exclusivity — Fairhaven asks you to commit

This island does not do love triangles. **Once you begin dating someone, no one else is romanceable.** If your heart truly changes, you must break up with your current partner — dating or married — before courting another. Flirt freely while unattached; commit deliberately.

> A small mercy for the indecisive: nothing stops you raising *friendship* with all eight before choosing. The hearts will be waiting when you decide.

## Practical Courtship Tips

- **Court through the stomach.** Every candidate keeps favorite dishes; a favorite gift moves hearts far faster than small talk. The [cooking pot](../crafting/cooking.md) is the most romantic station on your farm.
- **Chase their quests.** Romance cutscenes ride on quest completion — when your intended asks for something, drop the hoe and go.
- **Don't neglect the main story.** Story progress and new areas (Ravenwood Hollow especially) gate parts of the roster — see the [Story Overview](../story/overview.md).
- **Marrying Eury or Westley is a long game.** One arrives in Year 2, the other behind a hidden community. If you've fallen for a latecomer, spend Year 1 building friendships and fortune.
