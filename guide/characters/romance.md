# 💛 Romance Guide

Romance in *Wylde Flowers* is open-hearted by design: Tara may court any of
the eligible hearts **regardless of gender**, at her own pace, with the
story treating every pairing with the same warmth. It's one of the most
inclusive romance casts in the cozy genre, and the island is proud of it.

## How courtship works

Romance is friendship, continued with intent:

1. **Befriend them first.** Conversation, favors, and their favorite
   dishes — the [Gift Preferences](gift-preferences.html) page is
   effectively a dating guide with recipes.
2. **Watch for heart events.** As bonds deepen, personal story scenes
   arrive — histories, vulnerabilities, invitations. These scenes are the
   courtship; savor them.
3. **Declare intentions when the game offers.** Fairhaven is
   refreshingly direct: when the relationship is ready to become
   romantic, you'll be given the choice, plainly.
4. **Commitment follows in time** — up to and including settling down
   with your beloved. What that looks like, we leave you to discover.

## Gentle counsel

- **There's no wrong pick and no rush.** The story never forces a
  choice before you're ready, and every candidate's arc is worth seeing.
- **Explore freely, commit kindly.** You can get to know everyone —
  hearts on this island are sturdy — but when the game asks for
  exclusivity, it means it.
- **Their favorite foods are their biographies.** Every candidate's
  beloved dishes tell you where they come from; cook attentively and
  you're already halfway to understanding them.
- **Some romances are spoilers.** One name in the table above comes
  wrapped in late-game story. The journal says no more. 🤐
