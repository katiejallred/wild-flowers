# 🎁 Gift Preferences

The golden rule of Fairhaven gifting: **everyone wants food**, and every
resident keeps a short list of favorite dishes — usually three to five —
worth far more friendship than anything else you could hand them. The
lists are little biographies: each person's favorites trace their
heritage, their history, and their heart.

## Everyone else

Every other resident — Kai, Eury, and all the non-romanceable neighbors —
keeps favorites of their own, discoverable the pleasant way: pay
attention. Dialogue drops hints, quests reveal tastes, and a favorite
dish, once given, announces itself unmistakably in the reaction. As we
confirm more lists, this specimen sheet will grow.

## Gifting wisdom

- **Batch-cook.** A favorite dish is never a one-time gift; keep a stack
  for steady friendship-tending.
- **Read the favorites as recipes-to-hunt.** If a beloved dish isn't in
  your cookbook yet, its recipe becomes a quest of its own — check
  shops, friends, and the island's hidden caches.
- **Barn diplomacy counts too.** Your *animals* also have favorite
  foods, knowable via the crystal ball — the key to
  [magical animals](../farming/animals.html).
