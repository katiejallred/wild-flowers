# 🚜 Crop Guide

A pressed-and-labeled record of what grows in Fairhaven's soil, season by season. Remember the island's great mercy: seasons only change when the coven performs the ritual, so you may linger in any season as long as your ledger requires ([Farming 101](farming-101.md)). Just harvest everything before the ritual — out-of-season crops left in the ground vanish with the change.

> Specimens below are the ones this botanist has verified with her own trowel. Fairhaven's seed racks hold more than one journal page can press — treat these as the reliable core, not the complete flora.

---

## Spring Specimens

| Crop | Field notes |
| --- | --- |
| Turnip | The classic first sowing — quick, humble, dependable. |
| Potato | A sturdy staple; earthy, forgiving, and useful in many a kitchen recipe. |
| Cucumber | Crisp and cheerful; a good early companion for the pickle-minded. |

Spring is your tutorial season. Profits are modest across the board, so favor whatever keeps beds full and the [Cooking](../crafting/cooking.md) pot supplied.

## The Profitability Ledger

### Cotton, the quiet champion

**Cotton is the most profitable crop in the game — about 39 coins per day — but only once you turn it into cloth.** You cannot sell raw cotton at all; woven into cloth, one seed packet's worth returns around 273 coins, and the fabric sells to Kai. Cotton seeds come from Thomas at his farm (as do green beans), not the general store. See [Artisan Goods](../crafting/artisan-goods.md) for the processing side of the ledger.

### Ranked by coin-per-day

1. **Cotton → cloth** (~39/day) — best overall, requires processing.
2. **Fennel** (~29.5/day) — best straight-from-the-bed crop; fall and winter.
3. **Strawberry** (~23/day) — best of summer.
4. **Sunflower** — best-profit summer flower, with beehive dividends besides.

### Practical notes

- **The Greenhouse changes everything.** Once built (achievable by Winter of Year 1), seeds become available year-round — and since seasons only turn by ritual, you can simply *stay* in a profitable season while the greenhouse covers the rest.
- **Seeds:** crop seeds from Lina at the Dahl General Store, flower seeds from Violet, cotton and green beans from Thomas ([Shops](../island/shops.md)).
- **Selling isn't the only value.** Crops feed [Cooking](../crafting/cooking.md), [Potions](../witchcraft/potions.md), and gifts villagers adore ([Gift Preferences](../characters/gift-preferences.md)) — sometimes a carrot is worth more as a kindness.
- For the broader coin picture, see [Money-Making](../reference/money-making.md).

> Fruit trees are a separate investment entirely — one seedling, bought once, fruiting forever. Their page in this journal is [Beekeeping & Orchards](bees-and-orchards.md).
