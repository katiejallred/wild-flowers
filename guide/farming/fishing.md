# 🎣 Fishing

Fishing in Fairhaven is mercifully unfussy: no perfect-timing minigame
punishing your reflexes, no fish that only appears on rainy Tuesdays. Find
water, bring bait, be patient.

## Getting started

See **Bruno** at the fish and tackle shop in the middle of town, near the
statues. He'll hand you your first **rod** and a few pieces of **bait**, and
send you to the lake in the woods to learn the trade. From then on: stand by
water, cast, wait for the bite, reel.

## Bait

Three kinds of bait — **worms, crickets, and chub** — and different fish favor
different bait. If a spot keeps giving you the same catches, change bait
before you change location; the fish you're missing may simply be ordering
off a different menu.

## Where the fish live

Each body of water keeps its own family of fish:

| Waters | Where |
|---|---|
| **River fish** | The river on your farm |
| **Lake fish** | The lake in the woods |
| **Ocean fish** | The beach, and the shoreline south of town |
| **Mountain fish** | Mountain waters — home of the elusive salmon |
| **Mine fish** | Yes, there's water down there, and yes, things live in it |
| **Gloaming fish** | For witches only… you'll understand later 🌙 |

Two mercies for collectors: fish are **not** locked to time of day or season,
so filling the collection board is a matter of bait and patience, not
calendar-watching. And rare simply means rare — keep casting.

## Why fish at all?

- **Steady coin** from day one, with no seed money required.
- **Cooking ingredients** — many recipes want a fresh catch.
- **Surprises.** Ocean fishing occasionally hauls up more than fish —
  including cooking recipes. Salt water keeps secrets.
- **The collection board.** Around twenty boards on the island want filling,
  and the fish board is among the most satisfying.
