# 🤖 Whirligigs (Farm Robots)

> ⚠️ **Spoilers — Ravenwood Hollow.** The robots come from a shop in Ravenwood Hollow, the hidden community covered on the [Familiars](../witchcraft/familiars.md) page. This page names the shop and its owner, and nothing else about the Hollow.

A whirligig is a small flying robot that does one farm chore for you every day for as long as it has a charged gem in it. There are five: one each for watering, fishing, livestock, logging, and mining. Zephyr Grimspark builds them at her workshop, **Magical Mechanicals**, and she charges ingots for them and no coins.

## Getting the first one

The first whirligig is a quest reward. **Try Giving this a Whirl** starts the first time you water your crops in **Fall**, when Zephyr appears on the farm and tells you to stop watering by hand.

1. Visit Zephyr's workshop in Ravenwood Hollow.
2. Bring her **15 iron ingots, 15 copper ingots, and 10 silver ingots**.
3. Come back the next day. She delivers the **farming whirligig** to your farm for free and hands you **1 Powered Sapphire** to run it.

The farming whirligig waters your crops on its own, and it waters the greenhouse too once you have one. Finishing the quest also opens the rest of Zephyr's shop.

<span class="handwritten">the sapphire Zephyr gives you runs for 5 days, so bring her a second gem to charge before that first week is out 🌿</span>

## The five whirligigs

| Whirligig | What it does | What you supply | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Farming** | Waters your crops, greenhouse included | A powered gem | 15 iron, 15 copper, 10 silver ingots (the quest) |
| **Fishing** | Brings home fish | Bait and a powered gem | 15 iron, 15 copper, 5 gold ingots |
| **Livestock** | Feeds your chickens, cows, and sheep and collects what they produce | Enough feed for all your animals and a powered gem | 15 copper, 15 silver, 5 gold ingots |
| **Logging** | Brings home wood | A powered gem | 25 iron, 15 silver ingots |
| **Mining** | Goes into the mine and brings back ore and gems | A powered gem | 25 silver, 25 gold ingots |

All five together come to **55 iron, 45 copper, 65 silver, and 35 gold ingots**. The ore is in [the mine](the-mine.md), and Natalia smelts it into ingots at the forge.

The fishing whirligig's in-game description says you can tell it where to fish. You can't.

## Powered gems {#powered-gems}

A whirligig stops when its gem runs out. Zephyr charges a raw gem for you in exchange for the gem and one powder, and the better the gem, the longer it lasts.

| Powered gem | Runs a whirligig for | Zephyr needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Topaz | 1 day | 1 topaz + 1 bone meal or powdered calcium |
| Amethyst | 3 days | 1 amethyst + 1 bone meal or powdered calcium |
| Sapphire | 5 days | 1 sapphire + 1 bone meal or powdered calcium |
| Ruby | 7 days | 1 ruby + 1 pearl dust |
| Emerald | 10 days | 1 emerald + 1 pearl dust |
| Diamond | 14 days | 1 diamond + 1 pearl dust |

Two charged gems are free: chests on [mine floors](the-mine.md) 15 and 18 hold a powered amethyst and a powered emerald.

## Magical Mechanicals {#shop}

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Zephyr Grimspark |
| **Where** | Ravenwood Hollow |
| **Open** | Wednesday to Sunday, 9 am to 5 pm |
| **Closed** | Monday and Tuesday |
| **Prices** | Ingots, gems, and powders only, with no coin fee |

The shop is closed two days running, so have Zephyr charge your spare gems by Sunday if one is due to run out on a Monday or Tuesday.
