# 🚜 Farming 101

Hazel's farm is the anchor of Tara's new life, and its loop is the familiar,
comforting one: prepare a bed, plant a seed, water it daily, harvest, repeat —
now with more coin in your pocket than yesterday.

## The daily loop

1. **Water everything.** Un-watered crops simply pause; nothing dies of
   thirst, but nothing grows either. Watering is the one chore that anchors
   every morning.
2. **Harvest what's ready.** Some crops give one harvest; others keep
   producing once mature.
3. **Replant.** An empty bed earns nothing.
4. **Then the rest of the day is yours** — town, woods, mine, or mischief.

## Seeds and selling

**Dahl General Store** in the town square is your farming hub: it sells seeds
and produce, and it's the main place Tara sells her harvest. What's on the
seed shelf follows the current season — and since *you* eventually control
the season (see
[Energy, Time & Seasons](../getting-started/energy-time-seasons.html)), you
control the shelf too.

## Expanding the farm

The farm starts small and humble, and the story hands you the deed to change
that. Expect a steady drumbeat of projects, each wanting **wood, iron, and
coin** in some proportion:

- **More garden beds** — the single best early investment; more beds compound
  into everything else.
- **The chicken coop and the barn** — unlock animal husbandry; see
  [Animals & the Barn](animals.html).
- **The bridge to the hills** — opens more of your own land.
- **The tool shed** — grows into a full crafting workshop; see
  [Crafting Stations](../crafting/stations.html).
- **The hay field** — lets grazing animals feed themselves, retiring your
  daily hay bill.

Chop wood and mine ore whenever your energy would otherwise go unspent;
"materials in the chest" is the closest thing this game has to a savings
account.

## Beyond the basics

Once the beds hum along on their own momentum, farming branches into its
gentler specialties — [bees and orchards](bees-and-orchards.html),
[animals](animals.html) (including some *sparkly* ones), and the artisan
pipeline that turns raw goods into [cheese, wine, and cloth](../crafting/artisan-goods.html).
And one day, quite naturally, you'll find your farm chores interrupted by
witchcraft. Let it happen.
