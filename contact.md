# 💌 Contact the Guide

Spotted a sell price that's off? Found a secret the Guide missed? Or maybe you
just want to tell someone that you finally married Kai. Send a note below —
every message lands on my desk, and I read them all.

A few things that help:

- **Corrections:** paste the page link and tell me what you saw in your own
  game. The more specific the better — which season, which shop, which update.
- **Tips and secrets:** if a trick changed how you play, tell me about it. The
  best player tips find their way onto the pages, with your permission.
- **Game questions:** ask away. If the answer isn't in the Guide yet, it
  probably should be.
