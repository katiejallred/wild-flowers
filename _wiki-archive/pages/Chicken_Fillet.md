## Description

You can buy chicken fillets from Kim to use in recipes, or sell Kim grown [chickens](Chickens.md) you have raised.

## Notes

The day after selling your full-grown chicken to [Kim](Kim.md), they'll show up at your house and hand over three chicken fillets and some [feathers](Chicken_Feather.md)!
