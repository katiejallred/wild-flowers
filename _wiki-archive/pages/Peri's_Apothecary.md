*"Magical ingredients for your potioning and incantations."*

  

**Peri's Apothecary** is a shop in [Ravenwood Hollow](Ravenwood_Hollow.md) run by [Peri](Peri.md), who sells a rotating stock of forage items, crafting ingredients, potions, potion recipes, and seeds that can be grown in the [Gloaming](Gloaming.md). Peri also buys certain potions and magical flowers. Selling enough items to Peri unlocks new items available for purchase.
