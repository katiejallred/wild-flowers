## Acquaintance

**Trigger:** Find him in his store

[![Tara Wylde PT](../images/Tara_Wylde_PT.png)](Tara_Wylde.md)

So how long were you planning on letting me believe that you lived in Milkwater?

[![Westley Vuk PT](../images/Westley_Vuk_PT.png)](Westley_Vuk.md)

Well, it’s what the residents of Fairhaven have always assumed, so I just went with it!

[![Tara Wylde PT](../images/Tara_Wylde_PT.png)](Tara_Wylde.md)

Is it strange living among the fae, since you were born human?

[![Westley Vuk PT](../images/Westley_Vuk_PT.png)](Westley_Vuk.md)

Yes, I still feel like I have a lot to learn about fae customs and rules! I do miss having low-stakes human conversations sometimes. But I rather avoid going into town if it’s not necessary.

→  \[**Choice**: Just come by in the daytime.\]

[![Tara Wylde PT](../images/Tara_Wylde_PT.png)](Tara_Wylde.md)

You should just come by during the day. You won't have to worry about transforming into your wolfy self then.

[![Westley Vuk PT](../images/Westley_Vuk_PT.png)](Westley_Vuk.md)

That's true. I'll think about it.

→  \[**Choice**: How about I visit you more?\]

[![Tara Wylde PT](../images/Tara_Wylde_PT.png)](Tara_Wylde.md)

If you don’t want to come to town, I’ll visit you here more often! That way you still get some human interaction!

[![Westley Vuk PT](../images/Westley_Vuk_PT.png)](Westley_Vuk.md)

That’s kind of you. I would be delighted, and my samovar is always hot and ready to make a cup of tea!

→  \[**Choice**: Just come visit the coven.\]

[![Tara Wylde PT](../images/Tara_Wylde_PT.png)](Tara_Wylde.md)

Well if you're nervous about being around civilians, you could come visit the coven at our circle!

[![Westley Vuk PT](../images/Westley_Vuk_PT.png)](Westley_Vuk.md)

I'm not sure that's a good idea. You only meet at night, and I think I make some of your members uncomfortable when I'm in my wolf form. Plus, sometimes I have a hard time controlling my howling.

[![Westley Vuk PT](../images/Westley_Vuk_PT.png)](Westley_Vuk.md)

I hope you don’t mind my saying so, but I see a lot of Hazel in you. I hope you decide to stay on, and come visit us in Ravenwood Hollow whenever you need anything!

## Casual Friend

**Trigger:** After gaining two full hearts, speak to Westley on the bridge just to the left of his shop after he's closed for the day (around 7pm)

[![Tara Wylde PT](../images/Tara_Wylde_PT.png)](Tara_Wylde.md)

Evenin’ Westley! Hey, this looks like a good fishing spot!

[![Westley Vuk PT](../images/Westley_Vuk_PT.png)](Westley_Vuk.md)

Oh, I wouldn’t dare! Aryel would flip out if I hurt any wildlife in front of him! It’s honestly a bit weird for me. Back home, everyone I knew either fished or hunted. Salmon and game are such a big part of living in Alaska, you know? Oof, there’s that nostalgia. After I was, ah… bitten, I left home right away. I didn’t want to risk hurting my family. Some werefolks I met on the West Coast said that Peri might have potions that could help me, so I came here. Peri’s been great, but… she couldn’t un-werewolf me. So I don’t know if I’ll ever go home again.

→  \[**Choice**: Make your home here\]

[![Tara Wylde PT](../images/Tara_Wylde_PT.png)](Tara_Wylde.md)

I’m not saying it will ever truly make up for your loss, but maybe you could make this place your home? I never imagined I would feel so at home here when I first came to the island.

[![Westley Vuk PT](../images/Westley_Vuk_PT.png)](Westley_Vuk.md)

I hope you’re right. I guess I have made some friends since coming here, yourself included!

→  \[**Choice**: Home is acceptance\]

[![Tara Wylde PT](../images/Tara_Wylde_PT.png)](Tara_Wylde.md)

I think the right home for you is where people can accept you for being who you really are.

[![Westley Vuk PT](../images/Westley_Vuk_PT.png)](Westley_Vuk.md)

I agree, but I don't really feel that way here. I know I make Aryel uneasy and maybe the coven members too, when I'm in my wolf form.

→  \[**Choice**: Maybe you could go home\]

[![Tara Wylde PT](../images/Tara_Wylde_PT.png)](Tara_Wylde.md)

[![Westley Vuk PT](../images/Westley_Vuk_PT.png)](Westley_Vuk.md)

I should go fishing in Fairhaven more often, though. Nothing makes me feel at home like some fresh-caught salmon for dinner!

Dialogue

## Intimate Friend

**Trigger:** Visit Westley at his shop during business hours.

You ask him about his literary interests and the pair of you discuss folklore and fairytales. Westley asks you which is your favorite fairytale and confides a bit about his past. Afterwards, he admits he feels comfortable with you and appreciates your friendship.

[![Tara Wylde PT](../images/Tara_Wylde_PT.png)](Tara_Wylde.md)

Whatcha readin’, Westley?

[![Westley Vuk PT](../images/Westley_Vuk_PT.png)](Westley_Vuk.md)

Oh, I just acquired an out-of-print collection of traditional Russian fairy tales! I think my mom might have… softened the endings of some of these when she read them to me at bedtime!

[![Tara Wylde PT](../images/Tara_Wylde_PT.png)](Tara_Wylde.md)

Have you always loved old boos and lore?

[![Westley Vuk PT](../images/Westley_Vuk_PT.png)](Westley_Vuk.md)

Yes, I was studying the classics in college right before I got bit. I guess I was always kind of a dreamy kid. What about you, Tara? Do you have a favorite fairy tale?

→  \[**Choice**: Beauty and the Beast\]

[![Tara Wylde PT](../images/Tara_Wylde_PT.png)](Tara_Wylde.md)

I always loved Beauty and the Beast.

[![Westley Vuk PT](../images/Westley_Vuk_PT.png)](Westley_Vuk.md)

Hmmm… I wonder if true love could turn me into a prince?

→  \[**Choice**: The Twelve Dancing Princesses\]

[![Tara Wylde PT](../images/Tara_Wylde_PT.png)](Tara_Wylde.md)

→  \[**Choice**: Little Red Riding Hood\]

[![Tara Wylde PT](../images/Tara_Wylde_PT.png)](Tara_Wylde.md)

[![Westley Vuk PT](../images/Westley_Vuk_PT.png)](Westley_Vuk.md)

I guess part of my fascination with folktales is my hope that I might find a clue in them that will lead me to a cure. I never imagined when I used to read them that I would one day live among the fae. Peri doesn’t like us to spend too much time with humans but… I feel you can relate to how I feel. Your world changed just as suddenly as mine did.

[![Tara Wylde PT](../images/Tara_Wylde_PT.png)](Tara_Wylde.md)

That’s true.

[![Westley Vuk PT](../images/Westley_Vuk_PT.png)](Westley_Vuk.md)

Yes, well. I feel like I can be myself around you, Tara.

Dialogue

## Partner

**Trigger:** Visit Westley in front of his shop and he will ask you to catch a salmon. When you have it, he will invite you to meet him at the beach the next morning.

[![Westley Vuk PT](../images/Westley_Vuk_PT.png)](Westley_Vuk.md)

Sweetheart, if you have a moment, could you catch me some salmon? I want to make something special.

[![Tara Wylde PT](../images/Tara_Wylde_PT.png)](Tara_Wylde.md)

Well, you never know what’s going to bite, but I’ll try!

[![Westley Vuk PT](../images/Westley_Vuk_PT.png)](Westley_Vuk.md)

Is that fresh salmon? Perfect! Tomorrow morning, would you take a walk with me down by the beach? I’ll have your surprise ready then.

[![Tara Wylde PT](../images/Tara_Wylde_PT.png)](Tara_Wylde.md)

All right, what’s your scheme, honey?

[![Westley Vuk PT](../images/Westley_Vuk_PT.png)](Westley_Vuk.md)

I made you roast salmon the way my nana taught me! She used to tell me stories while she cooked. Do you want to hear one?

[![Tara Wylde PT](../images/Tara_Wylde_PT.png)](Tara_Wylde.md)

Of course I do!

[![Westley Vuk PT](../images/Westley_Vuk_PT.png)](Westley_Vuk.md)

Once upon a time, a man found a falcon’s feather and gave it to his youngest daughter, Alenushka. That night, an enormous falcon landed on Alenushka’s window. But when it saw her, the falcon turned into a man – Finist. Finist and Alenushka fell in love, but Alenushka’s jealous sisters put knives on her window. When Finist landed, he cried out and flew all the way back to the magical land where he lived. Alenushka went on a long journey to Finist’s land, where she finally found him and told him she hadn’t been the one to hurt him.

[![Tara Wylde PT](../images/Tara_Wylde_PT.png)](Tara_Wylde.md)

And then what? Did they live in Alenushka’s country or Finist’s?

[![Westley Vuk PT](../images/Westley_Vuk_PT.png)](Westley_Vuk.md)

None of the stories say. But I still like it. Nobody judges the fair maiden for loving someone who turns into an animal. And I want us to be able to make a life together, even if it’s hard, because I love you. This is that collection of Russian fairy tales I told you about. I want you to have it. \[Gives Book of Russian Fairy Tales\]

[![Tara Wylde PT](../images/Tara_Wylde_PT.png)](Tara_Wylde.md)

Oh, Westley, thank you. I love you, too. And for the record, I prefer werewolves to werefalcons.
