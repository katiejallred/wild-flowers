**A Class Act** is a [quest](To-Do_Quests.md) given by [Giva Joshi](Giva_Joshi.md) in the [epilogue](Epilogue.md). It requires the player to deliver 2x [Potato](Potato.md) and 2x [Cauliflower](Cauliflower.md) to her so she can make pav bhaji for a picnic with her new students. The reward for completion is 135 coins.

## Dialogue

Quest intro:

- Tara: What’s up, new teach?
- Giva: Oh, just thinking of how to restore the children’s faith in school now that their teacher’s been revealed as an egomaniacal kidnapper!
- Tara: You’re neither egomaniacal nor a kidnapped, so you should be fine.
- Giva: Right, but I should show them they can trust me. I was thinking maybe… a picnic? And you could help by providing fresh veggies?
- Tara: But Emmi and Finn hate fresh veggies, remember?
- Giva: Oh, please. I know that! I want to make pav bhaji with potatoes and cauliflower. It’s one of my favorite meals from Mumbai. You take vegetables and simmer them with spices until you get a mash, top it with butter, and… mmmmmmm.
- Tara: Ooh. Even the twins won’t turn up their noses at that.
- Giva: We’ll all go sit by the overlook, outside my house, and eat a meal together. It’ll be idyllic!
- Tara: In that case, I really can’t say no to bringing you potatoes and cauliflower.

Giva has asked for some potatoes and cauliflower.

- Giva: I brought all the spices I’d need for the pav bhaji from Kai. Now I just need the vegetables!
- Tara: And voila, here they are.
- Giva: Oh, this is perfect. Thanks, Tara! I’ll let you know how my picnic gambit works out.
- \[Reward: 135 coins\]
