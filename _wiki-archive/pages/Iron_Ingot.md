## Description

Iron ingots can be sold to Natalia or used to upgrade tools, build structures and craft items  
  
Iron Ingots are used to craft many of the buildable [workstations](Workstations.md) and upgrades around town

## Quests

Iron Ingot is used in the following [quests](Quests.md):

- A Barn in Stable Condition
