<td><img src="../images/Wylde_Flowers_Header.jpg" alt="Wylde Flowers Header" /> / <em>Wylde Flowers</em> casts you as <a href="Tara_Wylde.md">Tara Wylde</a>, a young girl from the city who's just moved to the island of <a href="Fairhaven.md">Fairhaven</a> to help out on her grandmother's farm. She's there to tend crops, fish, craft, cook, get to know the town's residents and maybe even find love. <em>Wylde Flowers</em> takes the formula that was popularized by games like Harvest Moon and Stardew Valley and gives it a supernatural twist. Shortly after Tara arrives in Fairhaven, she discovers that she's a witch. Her grandmother is part of a secret coven that meets in the woods. In addition to her farming duties and social life, Tara must also hone her magical abilities, brew potions and master flying on her broomstick.</td>
