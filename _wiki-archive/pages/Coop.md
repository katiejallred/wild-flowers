# Coop

*Categories: Farming, Animals*

*Source: <https://wylde-flowers.fandom.com/wiki/Coop>*

---

![There are multiple upgrades to the sizes of the coop.](../images/Coop.png)

*There are multiple upgrades to the sizes of the coop.*

The quest to gain the Coop is [All Cooped Up](All_Cooped_Up.md).

To upgrade the Coop, Tara must attempt to buy a [Duck](Duck.md) from [Marty Emerson](Marty_Emerson.md) on [The Hayseed Homestead](The_Hayseed_Homestead.md). Marty will say the Coop has to be expanded first and will unlock the Expanded Coop blueprint with [Parker](Parker.md) at [Nailed It!](Nailed_It!.md).

In order for [Ducks](Duck.md) to be available from Marty, Tara has to complete the Menagerie Mixup quest first. That quest triggers after completing the Upgraded [Greenhouse](Greenhouse.md) and the Propogation Shed, and potentially also the [Hay Field](Hay_Field.md).
