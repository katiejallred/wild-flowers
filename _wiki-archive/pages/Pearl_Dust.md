## Description

A dazzling dust with magical properties made by grinding pearls in the [Mortar and Pestle](Mortar_and_Pestle.md). Obtained from Lina during the wand upgrade quest.

## Quests

Pearl Dust is used in the following [quests](Quests.md): Zephyr will sell you powered gems, which requires one gem (each gem has a different time effect) and one Pearl Dust
