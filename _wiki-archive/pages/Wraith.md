# Wraith

*Categories: Wylde Flowers*

*Source: <https://wylde-flowers.fandom.com/wiki/Wraith>*

---

**Wraiths** are dangerous spirits born of the places where the Wellspring’s magic has been sucked dry. Some have been trapped inside the [Gloaming](Gloaming.md) for centuries. One of the responsibilities of the [coven](The_Fairhaven_Coven.md) is to keep the Wellspring balanced so they do not escape.

Wraiths can possess humans to influence their behavior, but it is possible to exorcise them using an incantation made with [Fennel Powder](Fennel_Powder.md), an item the possessed person made, and an item that carries an important memory to the possessed person.

The following quests involve exorcising wraiths:

- [Wraithing in Anticipation](Wraithing_in_Anticipation.md)
- [Seton’s Secrets](Seton’s_Secrets.md)
- [Forget Me Not](Forget_Me_Not.md)
- [Locket Up](Locket_Up.md)
