|             **Mon-Sun**              | 9:00am-6:30pm |

  
**Shelby's Scraps and Sundries** is a shop with rotating stock of forage items run by [Shelby Demeter](Shelby_Demeter.md). Shelby's business is accessed by speaking to him, no matter his location. He will sell to you on any day, at any time up until he returns to his cabin at 6:30pm. Selling more items to Shelby allows him to offer more stock each day.
