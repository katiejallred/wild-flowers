*"We take - and bake - the cake!"*

  

**Seton's Bakery** is a business run by [Angus Seton](Angus_Seton.md), located in the upper part of town next to his fiancé's [jewelry store](The_Jewelry_Box.md). He sells a variety of desserts and will happily buy Tara's, too!
