The Most Elaborate Icebreaker is a [Story Quest](To-Do_Quests.md) given by [Otto Soft](Otto_Soft.md). It requires you to speak to all 22 villagers at least once. The reward for completion is 30 coins.

  
**Quest:** Meet the mayor at [Town Hall](Town_Hall.md) in the center of town.  
**Hint:** When you get to town, keep following the path east until you see a big flag waving outside a set of open doors.

## Dialogue

#### Quest Intro

**Otto:** Oh, Hello! Welcome, welcome, welcome! I am Mayor Otto Soft – and you must be the new voter, I mean, neighbor!

**Tara:** Hi, and um, yes, that’s me.

**Otto:** If there is anything you need, I’m the one to speak to. We’re a friendly town here at Fairhaven! We want to make sure you are settled in well. To that end, we want you to meet everyone, so we’ve made it a little game! The fine Fairhaven folk will let me know after they’ve met you. That way, I can give you a prize after you’ve met everyone! It’s really quite clever and progressive.

**Tara:** Plus a little bit creepy.

**Otto:** Ah hahaha, you’re funny. Anyway, come and see me after you’ve met everyone!

#### Quest Completion

**Otto:** Wonderful! The last citizen has let me know that you’ve been introduced. As promised, here is your reward for playing our little game. We hope it helped you acclimatize.

**Tara:** Thanks! Money’s great too though.

## Reward

30x Coins
