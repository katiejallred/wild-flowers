**Meant To Bee** is a [quest](To-Do_Quests.md) given by [Violet Miranda](Violet_Miranda.md). It requires you to deliver 1x [Honey](Honey.md) to her. The reward for completion is 130 coins.

## Dialogue

Quest intro:

- Violet: Thanks again for helping me out with the thistles.
- Tara: No problem!
- Violet: Have you planted the tulip seeds I gave you? If you grow flowers in your garden beds, you can attract bees. First you need to build a beehive for them. Here are some instructions… If you keep the bees happy, they’ll make honey and beeswax. The more flowers, the faster the bees can produce homey, so plant plenty!
- Tara: Um, do I really want to keep bees, though? Won’t they sting?
- Violet: Bees can feel your intentions. You just have to come at them with the right energy. Come back when you have honey to sell and I’ll buy some from you.
- \[Receive Bee Hive blueprint\]

Quest completion:

- Violet: Did the bees make any honey yet?
- Tara: Bee-hold!
- Violet: See! I told you they wouldn’t sting if you stepped up nicely.
- Tara: Uh huh.
- Violet: Local honey is super popular. Sebastian drowns his pancakes in the stuff.
- Tara: And not in maple syrup? That’s a breakfast crime!
- Violet: I know. Weird, right? But our mom used to eat pancakes like that, too. My guess is, it’s his way of honoring her.
- Tara: Did your mom… pass away?
- Violet: No. Um, I don’t think so. Listen, here are some more tulip seeds. \[Gives 3x Tulip seeds.\]
- Tara: Sorry, should I not have-
- Violet: The more you plant, the more likely you are to attract bees! A win-win.
- \[Reward: 130 coins\]
