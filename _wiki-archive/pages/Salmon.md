## Description

This fish is famous for swimming upstream to spawn. Found near the mountains. A rare catch! Can be used in recipes or sold to Bruno for a nice profit.

## Other information

Salmon is one of the [Cat](Cleocatra.md)'s favorite gifts.

Although [Bruno](Bruno.md) tells you to be sure to bring [crickets](Cricket.md) when fishing for salmon, it can actually be caught with any bait!
