## Unlock

Before Tara is able to raise ducks she must help [Marty Emerson](Marty_Emerson.md) provide enough feed to his livestock transportation shipments via the Menagerie Mixup quest. Tara must also have the Upgraded [Greenhouse](Greenhouse.md) and the Expanded [Coop](Coop.md).

## Raising Ducks

Ducks can be purchased from [Marty](Marty.md) at [The Hayseed Homestead](The_Hayseed_Homestead.md). They take one full week to grow from ducklings into adult ducks, at which point they will start laying one [egg](Duck_Egg.md) per day. Though all ducklings are identical, adult ducks may be varied!

Ducks require one serving of [chicken feed](Chicken_feed.md) per day and can be pet to improve Tara's relationship with them. Ducks who have not yet been fed will have an exclamation mark over their head (similar to the appearance of villagers with quests available). After **\#** days of not being fed, a duck will fall ill and need to be cured using a [Cure Animal Sickness Potion](Cure_Animal_Sickness_Potion.md) (unlocked through a questline with [Aryel](Aryel.md)).

  

## Selling

Having a good relationship with your ducks does not influence the amount of eggs they lay; rather, it increases the price for which you can sell the ducks to [Kim](Kim.md) or [Marty](Marty.md). (Note: [Kim](Kim.md) will not buy ducks before they've matured). Feeding a duck its favorite food every day will result in [Sparkly Duck Eggs](Sparkly_Duck_Egg.md).
