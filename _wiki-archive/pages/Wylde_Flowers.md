**Wylde Flowers** is a cozy farm sim developed and published by Studio Drydock, a game studio based in Australia. Players take on the role of [Tara Wylde](Tara_Wylde.md), who travels to [Fairhaven](Fairhaven.md) to help her grandmother. Very soon Tara discovers she has a gift and that she is an actual witch. During her adventure, she will learn new skills and make many new friends.
