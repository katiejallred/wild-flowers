Juliet Moretti (13) is the daughter of Sophia and the recently deceased Giacomo. She's shy and easily embarrassed, but a talented guitarist and painter.

She also shows promise as a healer, but she can't imagine sitting through all the school that becoming a doctor would take. In the end of the game it was revealed that her father was a witch as she received his wand and wish to join the coven one day after she discover she was a witch too.
