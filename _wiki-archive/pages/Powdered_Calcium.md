## Description

Ground-up seashells in a [Mortar and Pestle](Mortar_and_Pestle.md). Used for crafting (and building strong bones).

## Quests

Powdered Calcium is used in the following [quests](Quests.md):

- [Amira](Amira.md) occasionally requests powdered calcium on the [Bulletin Board](Bulletin_Board_Quests.md)
