## Description

Drink this potion to replenish some of your magic.

## Other information

This potion is only available to purchase, not for crafting. A stronger version of the potion, the [Max Restore Magic Potion](Max_Restore_Magic_Potion.md), can be crafted at the [Cauldron](Cauldron.md).
