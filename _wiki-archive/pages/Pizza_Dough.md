## Description

Can be used to make a variety of pizza recipes.

## Other information

Pizza dough is a cooked item but can't be eaten.

The recipe for pizza dough is included in the book [Recipes for Pizza Chefs](Recipes_for_Pizza_Chefs.md), sold at [Cafe Moretti](Cafe_Moretti.md).
