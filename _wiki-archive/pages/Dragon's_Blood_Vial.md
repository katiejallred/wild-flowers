## Description

A vial of red sap used as a powerful ingredient in magical crafting. No dragons were harmed in the making of this vial.

## Quests

Dragon's Blood Vial is used in the following [quests](Quests.md):

- A [Bulletin Board quest](Bulletin_Board_Quests.md) for [Aryel](Aryel.md) calls for 5 Dragon's Blood Vials.
