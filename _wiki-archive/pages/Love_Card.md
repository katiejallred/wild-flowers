## Description

"A romantic card made with flower paper that features a custom, heartfelt message." Can appear as a Bulletin Board Quest for any townie/non-romanceable character in a relationship ([Angus](Angus.md), [Francis](Francis.md), [Thomas](Thomas.md), [Sophia](Sophia.md), [Parker](Parker.md), [Lina](Lina.md)), which asks someone (the player) to secretly deliver a Love Card to their significant other.
