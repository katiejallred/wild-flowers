# Season's Greetings

*Categories: Quests*

*Source: <https://wylde-flowers.fandom.com/wiki/Season%27s_Greetings>*

---

**Season's Greetings** is a [quest](To-Do_Quests.md) given by the [High Priestess](The_Fairhaven_Coven.md) during the [Fall](Fall.md) after finishing the quest [Know Witch Way the Wind Blows](Know_Witch_Way_the_Wind_Blows.md). It requires you to craft 1x [Winter Ritual Incantation](Winter_Ritual_Incantation.md) and 1x [Spirit Flame](Spirit_Flame.md). The reward for completion is 5x [Revitalizing Potion](Revitalizing_Potion.md).

## Dialogue

Quest intro:

- Damon: I can’t stop thinking about Sophia. She had to suffer for something we messed up.
- ([Wraithing in Anticipation](Wraithing_in_Anticipation.md) completed)
  - Damon: At least Tara and Amira got Natalia to quit spreading that skinder.
  - High Priestess Lina: You did Fairhaven – and Sophia – a great service. Thank you both. 
- ([Wraithing in Anticipation](Wraithing_in_Anticipation.md) not completed)
  - Damon: And now Otto can say his neighborhood watch is important because it flushed out a witch! If only he knew...

- Farseer Shelby: But all that trying amounted to a hill of beans. The wraith still got out.

- High Priestess Lina: It’s true. We have to do a better job of keeping this island safe. But for now, let us turn our attention to the changing of the seasons. It won’t be easy this time.
