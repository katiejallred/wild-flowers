# Gifting

*Categories: Game Mechanics*

*Source: <https://wylde-flowers.fandom.com/wiki/Gifting>*

---

Giving people gifts always increases friendship slightly, but giving someone one of their Favorite Gifts provides a huge boost! (But only once per item, then it's only a bit more than other gifts).

As of V1.5, farm animals have a favorite food. It is random and unique to each individual animal. Using a crystal ball on them will narrow down the choices to a particular season's crops or type of crop, such as fruit.
