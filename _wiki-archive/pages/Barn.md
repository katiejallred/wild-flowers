# Barn

*Categories: Needs info, Animals, Farming*

*Source: <https://wylde-flowers.fandom.com/wiki/Barn>*

---

The **Barn** is a space where Tara can raise cows for [milk](Milk.md) and sheep for [wool](Wool.md). It is unlocked after restoring the [chicken coop](Chickens.md) and completing the upgrade quest titled A Barn in Stable Condition. The upgrade costs 120 [wood](Wood.md), 60 [Iron Ingots](Iron_Ingot.md), and 1800 coins and takes 2 days to complete.

Cows and sheep both require one serving of [hay](Hay.md) per day until Tara builds the <a href="https://wylde-flowers.fandom.com/wiki/Hay_Field?action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="text" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Hay Field</a>, which allows the animals to graze. Animals who have not yet been fed will have an exclamation mark over their head (similar to the appearance of villagers with quests available). After 1 day of not being fed, an animal will fall ill and need to be cured using a [Cure Animal Sickness Potion](Cure_Animal_Sickness_Potion.md) (unlocked through a questline with [Aryel](Aryel.md).)

Tara can also pet them to improve their relationship with them. Having a good relationship with your animals does not influence the amount or quality of products produced; rather, it increases the price for which you can sell them to [Kim](Kim.md) or [Marty](Marty.md). (Note: [Kim](Kim.md) will not buy baby animals)

## Cows

![An adult cow!](../images/Cow.png)

*An adult cow!*

Calves can be purchased from [Marty](Marty.md) for 1500 coins and mature to adulthood in one week. Each adult cow produces at least one [milk](Milk.md) per day.

## Sheep

![A full-grown sheep](../images/Sheep.png)

*A full-grown sheep*

Sheep can be purchased from [Marty](Marty.md) for 1200 coins and mature to adulthood in 8 days. Each adult sheep produces at least one [wool](Wool.md) every 3 days.

## Quotes

When approaching the ruins of the barn or coop for the first time, the following cutscene plays:

> Gramma: Ah, I see you’ve found the barn.
>
> Tara: This is it? I went looking for it but I wasn’t sure. Remember the time I milked Bessie, and she kicked over the milk bucket? I hid in the barn because I thought you would be mad!
>
> Gramma: Haha, no use crying over spilled milk! Or a barn that got blown down in a storm, I suppose. I had to give up keeping cattle.
>
> Tara: So, does that mean this is…
>
> Gramma: Yep. The chicken coop. After Merlin passed, I didn’t have a dog to guard the chickens and they kept getting picked off. I chopped up the coop for firewood.
>
> Tara: Oh. That’s a shame. Do you think we could restore them?
>
> Gramma: I’m sure we could, but after that old ranch closed up, the nearest place to get more animals is miles away. If Mayor Otto would bestir himself to do something about that then fixing up these buildings would be light work for Parker.
