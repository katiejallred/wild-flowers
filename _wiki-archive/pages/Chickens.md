## Unlock

Before Tara is able to raise chickens she must help [Mayor Soft](Otto.md) restore the ranch next to [Thomas's](Thomas.md) farm (costing 80x [wood](Wood.md), 50x [stone](Stone.md), 10x [iron ingots](Iron_Ingot.md), and 1000 coins) and have [Parker](Parker.md) restore the chicken coop on her own property (for 100x [wood](Wood.md), and 1000 coins). Restoring the chicken coop requires you to first clear out the surrounding area and so a [copper pickaxe](Copper_Pickaxe.md) and (at least) a [copper wood axe](Copper_Wood_Axe.md) are also required. You can also get [eggs](Egg.md) from [Dahl General Store](Dahl_General_Store.md).

## Raising Chickens

Chickens can be purchased from [Marty](Marty.md) at [The Hayseed Homestead](The_Hayseed_Homestead.md) for 250 coins. They take one full week to grow from chicks into adult chickens, at which point they will start laying one [egg](Egg.md) per day. Though all chicks are identical, adult chickens may be white, brown, or black!

![Chick Buy Screen](../images/Chick_Buy_Screen.png)

Chickens require one serving of [chicken feed](Chicken_feed.md) per day and can be pet to improve Tara's relationship with them. Chickens who have not yet been fed will have an exclamation mark over their head (similar to the appearance of villagers with quests available). After **\#** days of not being fed, a chicken will fall ill and need to be cured using a [Cure Animal Sickness Potion](Cure_Animal_Sickness_Potion.md) (unlocked through a questline with [Aryel](Aryel.md)).

  

## Selling

Having a good relationship with your chickens does not influence the amount or quality of eggs they lay; rather, it increases the price for which you can sell them to [Kim](Kim.md) or [Marty](Marty.md). (Note: [Kim](Kim.md) will not buy chicks before they've matured)
