## Unlock

Before Tara is able to raise pigs she must help [Marty Emerson](Marty_Emerson.md) provide enough feed to his livestock transportation shipments via the Menagerie Mixup quest. Tara must also have the Upgraded [Greenhouse](Greenhouse.md).

## Raising Pigs

Pigs can be purchased from [Marty](Marty.md) at [The Hayseed Homestead](The_Hayseed_Homestead.md). They take one full week to grow from piglets into adult pigs, at which point they will start providing [Truffles](Truffle.md) when pet each day. Though all piglets are identical, adult pigs may be varied!

Pigs require one serving of [Hay](Hay.md) per day and can be pet to improve Tara's relationship with them. Pigs who have not yet been fed will have an exclamation mark over their head (similar to the appearance of villagers with quests available). After **\#** days of not being fed, a pig will fall ill and need to be cured using a [Cure Animal Sickness Potion](Cure_Animal_Sickness_Potion.md) (unlocked through a questline with [Aryel](Aryel.md)). Once a [Hay Field](Hay_Field.md) is built, pigs will feed themselves each day.
