**Marty** is the new ranch owner in town and has also moved to [Fairhaven](Fairhaven.md) from the city. His self-confidence and excitement at starting a ranch seem to outweigh his actual ranching skills.

Most of the townspeople are suspicious of his intentions, and his interactions with Tara suggest he’s hiding something. Although Mayor Soft handpicked Marty to run the ranch, they rarely see eye to eye and Marty ends up running for Mayor in the Fairhaven election.

Marty raises and sells livestock which the player can purchase for their farm after building the chicken coop or barn.

## Life Before Fairhaven

Not much is known about Marty’s life before moving to Fairhaven. He mentions he was a businessman but hasn't said what kind of business he did - something about consulting?

Upon his first introduction to Tara, he’s about to use a last name starting with 'Ma-', before using Emerson, suggesting that he may have gotten into trouble in the city and is starting over in Fairhaven.

**Click to reveal spoilers!**

Marty's real name is Martin Maplethorpe. After Martin's father ran the family real estate business into the ground, the younger Maplethorpe decided to assume a new identity: bumbling rancher Marty Emerson. His real goal wasn't raising cattle, but scouting locations for a bayside resort. And he might have gotten away with it, if it weren't for that meddling mayor.
