# Fishing

*Categories: Fishing*

*Source: <https://wylde-flowers.fandom.com/wiki/Fishing>*

---

**Fishing** is unlocked when [Bruno Soft](Bruno_Soft.md) gives the quest [Lure One In](Lure_One_In.md), giving Tara a [fishing rod](Fishing_Rod.md).

  
There are 6 categories of fish, reflecting 7 areas with at least one fishing spot:

- **River** fish are caught in the [Farm](Farm.md) area
- **Lake** fish are caught in the [Woods](Woods.md)
- **Ocean** fish are caught in the [Beach](Beach.md) area and to the south of the [Town Center](Town_Center.md)
- **[Mountain](Mountain.md)**, **[Mine](Mine.md)**, and **[Gloaming](Gloaming.md)** fish are found in the areas of the same names

Different fish can be caught using the three types of bait: [chub](Chub.md), [crickets](Cricket.md), and [worms](Worm.md).

Some cooking recipes can be caught when a bottle appears in the water after casting the line. The recipe is unlocked when the bottle is reeled in like a fish, which uses up the bait.
