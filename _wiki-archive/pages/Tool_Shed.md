## Tool Shed

The tool shed is a space next to Tara's farmhouse where she can build a variety of crafting stations to refine resources. Originally 5x6, it can be upgraded by [Parker Johnson](Parker_Johnson.md) to have a useable layout of 7x12 (total size 9x12). The upgrade costs 50x [wood](Wood.md) and 200 coins and requires Tara to clear the surrounding space, which may call for a [wood axe](Tools.md) and/or [pickaxe](Tools.md). The upgrade will be complete on the second morning after purchasing.

## Quotes

When entering the tool shed for the first time, the following cutscene plays:

> Gramma: You know, I don’t think I’ve been in here since your Grampa died.
>
> Tara: Sorry, I was just looking around like you said. I didn’t mean to pry.
>
> Gramma: Oh, no, no, it’s fine, I just wasn’t as good with my hands as he was. Gave most of his tools to Thomas, since I knew he’d put them to good use. If we want to get this place up and running, though, we’ll need to put in the work here, too. We always made a whole lot more money from his crafts than just the farm produce alone. If you ever want to try your hand at it, I kept the blueprints to all his tools right here.
>
> Tara: Thanks, Gramma. Not sure if I’m a handy-Tara, but I’ll try for us!
