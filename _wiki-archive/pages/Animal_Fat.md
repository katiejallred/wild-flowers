## Description

Animal byproduct. Cook with it or make [tallow candles](Tallow_Candle.md) using the [candlemaker](Candlemaker.md).

## Other information

Animal fat can be obtained by selling a cow or a sheep to [Kim's Local Butcher and Deli](Kim's_Local_Butcher_and_Deli.md). The reward for selling a cow includes 2 Animal Fat, while the reward for a sheep includes 3.

  
This ingredient can be used in a handful of recipes that accept either Animal Fat or [Olive Oil](Olive_Oil.md) as their fat.
