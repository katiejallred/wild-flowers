## Description

A dense, vivid flower that grows in [Summer](Summer.md). Use it as a crafting ingredient or sell it to [Violet](Violet_Miranda.md). Marigold crops that are fully grown but not harvested contribute to any nearby [Bee Hive](Bee_Hive.md)'s production of [Honey](Honey.md).
