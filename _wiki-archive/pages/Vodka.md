## Description

Vodka is made in a [distillery](Distillery.md) with potatoes. Drinking it replenishes some magic but costs some energy. Can be sold to Damon and used in cocktail recipes.  
  

## Favorite Gift

Vodka is one of [Natalia](Natalia.md)'s favorite gifts.
