Violet Miranda (24) is [Sebastián's](Sebastián_Miranda.md) sister and the owner of the [flower shop](Violet's_Violets.md). She's excitable, and as colorful and lively as her name.

Violet likes fashion and wants to open a boutique one day.

**Click to reveal spoilers!**

When Violet was a child, her father, Antonio Miranda, was the mayor of [Fairhaven](Fairhaven.md).

  
She enjoyed her life on the island until her mother, [Gloria](Gloria_Miranda.md), disappeared without a trace. Violet wants to let go of her painful past, which might mean leaving [Fairhaven](Fairhaven.md), but that's a big step.

Within the coven, Violet was just promoted from Acolyte to Herbalist, a title that recognizes her outstanding talent for potion-brewing.

## Other Information

Violet is usually in her shop, but can sometimes be found standing by Marty's ranch or sitting on bench in front of the town hall.
