A **Garden Bed** is a structure in the [Garden](Garden.md) that allows crops, flowers, and trees to be grown. Seeds that are planted in a garden bed and watered each day will produce crops. Each garden bed will produce 4 crops unless a production-boosting potion is used.

For a full list of products grown in garden beds, see: [Garden](Garden.md).
