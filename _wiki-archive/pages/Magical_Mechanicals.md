*"If you need something built that has sparkles, this is the place"*

  

[Zephyr's](Zephyr_Grimspark.md) workshop is the only suitable place to go to fulfill your magical and mechanical needs! Most importantly, this is where you can unlock new "whirligigs" and obtain powered gems to keep them going. Unlike the [Blacksmith](Blacksmith.md), [Nailed It!](Nailed_It!.md), and crafting station builds, these purchases cost *only* resources -- no coins required! Unlock the workshop by completing the [Try Giving this a Whirl](Try_Giving_this_a_Whirl.md) quest and unlocking the Farming Whirligig.
