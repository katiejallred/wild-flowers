Lina Dahl-Johnson (40) runs the general store, [Dahl General Store](Dahl_General_Store.md) alongside her husband, [Parker](Parker_Johnson.md). She's also the mother of twins [Emmi](Emmi_Johnson.md) and [Finn](Finn_Johnson.md).

She is dependable and professional, but sometimes feels like the responsibilities of running the family business have left her with little time for anything else.

**Click to reveal spoilers!**

That, alongside with her status as a witch, puts strain on her marriage to Parker.  
  
Lina is the High Priestess of the Fairhaven coven, a responsibility she takes very seriously. She's studied magic ever since she was a child, and her father's guidance, mixed with her steady determination to be the best, has made her one of the most capable witches Fairhaven has ever seen.

## Life Before Fairhaven

Lina used to babysit Tara when she was little. Her family has lived in Fairhaven for hundreds of years. Her father once ran the general store, but now he lives in a retirement home in Milkwater.
