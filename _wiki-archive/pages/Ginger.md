Ginger is a Corgi that Tara can adopt in Year 3. As of the [Magical Creatures Update](Patch_Notes.md), she is the final gift-able character in the game. Like [Cleocatra](Cleocatra.md), she you can grow your relationship to pet and later familiar as you gain hearts.
