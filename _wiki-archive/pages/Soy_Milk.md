## Description

You can make soy milk out of soybeans with a [juicer](Juicer.md) in the tool shed, after you earn the recipe from [Thomas Lightfoot](Thomas_Lightfoot.md). Soy milk can be used to make [Tofu](Tofu.md) or candles.
