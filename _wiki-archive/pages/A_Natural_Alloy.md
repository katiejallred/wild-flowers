**A Natural Alloy** is a [quest](To-Do_Quests.md) given by [Francis Calderón](Francis_Calderón.md). It requires you to deliver 2x[Silver Ingot](Silver_Ingot.md) to him so he can make wedding bands for himself and [Angus Seton](Angus_Seton.md). The reward for completion is 145 coins.

## Dialogue

Quest intro:

- Francis: Just the miner I was hoping to see! Have you struck silver in them thar hills yet?
- Tara: You know it’s not hills, right?
- Francis: Of – of course. Sure. Mostly. Looks, I love working with precious metals, but I have no idea how to extract them. And I need some silver for our wedding bands. If you dig up some raw ore, Natalia can turn it into an ingot that I can work with.
- Tara: Let’s see what I can find. You and Angus deserve the most beautiful rings!

Quest completion:

- Tara: Is this the kind of silver you need?
- Francis: Oh, Tara. This is perfect. I can’t thank you enough.
- Tara: Just give me first look at the rings when you’re done! They’re going to be stunning.
- Francis: Here, this is for you. Thanks so much!
- \[Reward: 145 coins\]
