**Revenge is Sweet** is a [quest](To-Do_Quests.md) given by the [Journeyman](Damon_Mthembu-Haas.md) during the [Fall](Fall.md). It requires you to cook 1x [Pumpkin Pie](Pumpkin_Pie.md) and then brew 1x [Pranking Pumpkin Pie](Pranking_Pumpkin_Pie.md) in order to try to make [Otto Soft](Otto_Soft.md) cluck like a chicken during the mayoral debate. The reward for completion is 3x [Miracle Grow Potion](Miracle_Grow_Potion.md).

This quest is optional. Completing it before the mayoral debate that takes place **\[x\]** days before the election will alter some of the dialogue during the debate and many of the villagers will comment about it afterwards, but it will not affect the outcome of the election.

## Dialogue

#### Quest Intro

**Journeyman Damon:** You heard about the debate coming up?

**Tara:** I sure did. I didn’t think towns this small had political debates…

**Journeyman Damon:** Trust me. Growing up in Fairhaven taught me that there’s no town too small for local election drama. And speaking of drama… I have an idea that’ll make the debate unforgettable.

![ConversationIcon](../images/ConversationIcon.png) Out with it

**Tara:** This should be good. Let’s hear it.

**Journeyman Damon:** I knew you’d be interested!

![ConversationIcon](../images/ConversationIcon.png) Sounds like bad news

**Tara:** Could this idea be described as "unethical?"

**Journeyman Damon:** Have you met Otto Soft? Ethics are out the window already, bruv!

**Journeyman Damon:** I don’t want to hurt Otto, even though he’s hurt my business by doubling liquor taxes. I just think it’d be good for the guy to eat a slice of humble pie. Which, if eaten before the debate, will make him cluck like the chicken he is.

**Tara:** And you want me to make this humble pie, right?

**Journeyman Damon:** Yah, but it’s a special pie. It’s got a potion-

**Tara:** I know, Journeyman. It was figurative.

**Journeyman Damon:** So are you in?

![ConversationIcon](../images/ConversationIcon.png) Sure

**Tara:** Why not? It’ll be funny.

**Journeyman Damon:** Ahhh! Let’s get to work, Novice. You’ll need bones, some silver essence, and to bake a pumpkin pie. Brew that up and bring me the draught. I’ll take care of the rest. Hehe.

![ConversationIcon](../images/ConversationIcon.png) Nope

**Tara:** This isn't fair to Otto.

**Journeyman Damon:** You're right, but don't remind me! That's less fun. Guess I'll just ask someone else for help...

#### Quest Completion

**Journeyman Damon:** The debate’s coming up. Are you almost done with that brew?

**Tara:** I have it right here.

**Journeyman Damon:** Hazel… Hazel would be so proud of you.

**Tara:** You really think she’d be alright with this?

**Journeyman Damon:** Oh, yah. When I was a kid, she gave the ferry captain bunny ears for a month because he was rude to my ma. Keep your eyes on Otto during the debate!
