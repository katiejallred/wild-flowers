# The Fairhaven Summer Festival

*Categories: Quests*

*Source: <https://wylde-flowers.fandom.com/wiki/The_Fairhaven_Summer_Festival>*

---

**The Fairhaven Summer Festival** is a [quest](To-Do_Quests.md) delivered by letter from [Otto Soft](Otto_Soft.md) at the beginning of [Summer](Summer.md). It begins a 15-day countdown to the Summer Festival. The quest has no objectives, but can be checked at any time so see the number of days left until the festival.

## Letter Contents

- \[constituent’s name here\]
- You are cordially invited to attend the annual Fairhaven Summer festival.
- It’s in just a few weeks!
- Hope to see you there!
- Sincerely,
- Mayor Otto Soft
