# Gloaming

*Categories: Locations*

*Source: <https://wylde-flowers.fandom.com/wiki/Gloaming>*

---

The Gloaming is a location that can be reached by magic.

| The Gloaming is a magical realm that can be reached after repairing the standing stones in the [woods](Woods.md). It does not have any inhabitants but is full of magical plants and spiderwebs. To move around the place, magic must be used against the writhing vines and magic barriers and a [spirit flame potion](Spirit_Flame.md) is needed to get past the spiderwebs. In the Gloaming, you can find a wellspring, a watering well and several flowerbeds that can be used to grow magical plants. The Gloaming is also where [Echo Blossoms](Echo_Blossom.md) can be found, which contain messages from [Violet's mother](Gloria_Miranda.md) and reveal how she vanished. |
