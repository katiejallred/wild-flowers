**Bee Hives** are structures placed in the [Garden](Garden.md) that produce [Honey](Honey.md) and [Beeswax](Beeswax.md) over time. Production time depends on how many fully-grown flowers are planted per bee hive.

Daily production requires a ratio of 12 Flowers:1 Hive.
