**A Fab-Yule-Us Feast** is a [quest](To-Do_Quests.md) given by [Kim Izumi](Kim_Izumi.md) at the beginning of [Winter](Winter.md). It requires the player to deliver 5x [Shiitake Mushroom](Shiitake_Mushroom.md) to them so they can cook [Beef & Shiitake](Beef_&_Shiitake.md) for the [Winter Feast](Winter_Feast.md). The reward for completion is 3x [Pork](Pork.md).

## Dialogue

Quest intro:

- Tara: Mornin, Kim!
- Kim: Hi, Tara. Mrs. Soft has given me the, um… honor of organizing the Winter Feast this year. See, every household contributes something, and we all gather together for a feast on the shortest day of Winter.
- Tara: What a nice tradition!
- Kim: Sophia used to run things, but, well…
- Tara: Right.
- Kim: So. I’m making a roast with a shiitake mushroom sauce. I hear shiitake grow in the mine, but… I don’t do small enclosed spaces. Not at all.
- Tara: Don’t worry, I’m on it.
- Kim: Thanks.

Quest completion:

- Kim: Find any shiitake yet?
- Tara: Yes! Here they are.
- Kim: You’re my hero. Seriously, there’s just something about dark caves where like, anything could be in there with you… Like bats. Or wolves.
- Tara: I hadn’t actually thought about that, but I will now! So… thank you.
- Kim: Always good to keep a look out.
- \[Reward: 3 Pork\]
