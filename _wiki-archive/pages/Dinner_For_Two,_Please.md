# Dinner For Two, Please

*Categories: Quests*

*Source: <https://wylde-flowers.fandom.com/wiki/Dinner_For_Two%2C_Please>*

---

**Dinner For Two, Please** is a [quest](To-Do_Quests.md) given by [Sophia Moretti](Sophia_Moretti.md) after a potential love interest invites you to dinner after fully filling 5 hearts. It requires you to deliver 2x [Mushroom](Mushroom.md) and 1x [Tuna](Tuna.md) to her, which she will use to cook a romantic dinner the next evening.

## Dialogue

Letter received after being invited to dinner:

- Well, Tara!
- I’ve heard someone is taking a special interest in you! Come and see me…
- --Sophia

Talk to Sophia to find out her plans for your date!

- Tara: I got your letter…
- Sophia: I’ll put on a romantic dinner for you and \[character\]. Just bring me some ingredients! Some mushrooms and a tuna should do it!

Collect some mushrooms and tuna and give them to Sophia so she can cook you and your date an intimate dinner.

- Sophia: Have you got the ingredients?
- Tara: I sure have.
- Sophia: Great! Come to the diner tomorrow night and I’ll have everything prepared.

Wait until tomorrow night for Sophia to prepare a romantic dinner.
