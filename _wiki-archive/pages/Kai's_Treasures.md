*"Exotic goods from across the bay."*

  

Kai's Treasures is a stall run by [Kai Hoapili](Kai_Hoapili.md), located in the center of the town square next to [Dahl General Store](Dahl_General_Store.md). It stocks a variety of treasures ranging from fruit trees to spices; villager dialogue frequently mentions that Kai gets his treasures from off-island, presumably by surfboard!
