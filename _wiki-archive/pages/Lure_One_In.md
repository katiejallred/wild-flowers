Lure One In is a [Story Quest](To-Do_Quests.md) given by [Bruno Soft](Bruno_Soft.md) that serves as a tutorial for [Fishing](Fishing.md). It requires you to first catch any three fish, then to catch a [Sea Bass](Sea_Bass.md). You receive 3x [Chub](Chub.md) for catching three fish, and 3x [Chub](Chub.md), 3x [Worm](Worm.md), and 3x [Cricket](Cricket.md) for catching a sea bass. The reward for completion is the [Fish Stew](Fish_Stew.md) recipe.

## Dialogue

#### Quest Intro

**Bruno:** Your grandma was always good luck on a boat. Had a real good eye for spotting fish.

**Tara:** But… How can you see fish if they’re underwater?

**Bruno:** She hasn’t taught ya much, has she?

**Tara:** I… erm… potatoes…

**Bruno:** Here. Take this rod. Keep yer eyes peeled whenever you’re near water for the shadows of swimmin’ fish. When you see ‘em near the surface like that, ya know they’re feedin’. Trick is to get one of ‘em to nibble on yer hook instead of the baitfish they’re chasin’.

**Tara:** How do I do that?

**Bruno:** You’ll need some bait! Here are some worms. You’re not the squeamish type, are ya? Ya gotta hook yer bait and cast yer line. You’ll feel when you’ve got one and then reel it in!

**Tara:** Wait… back up… how do I cast my line?

**Bruno:** You’ll get the hang of it. The lake in the forest is a nice place to start. But take care, ya hear? Don’t go in those woods after sunset. I’ve seen things… Well, ya wouldn’t believe me. My boy Otto didn’t either ‘til he saw it for himself! Just stay safe, and come back and see me after ya’ve given it a few tries.

#### Part 1

Bruno has asked you to practice fishing by catching any fish.

**Bruno:** Wonderful! You’ve got the look of a life-long angler in yer eyes now! Now here’s a real challenge. Catchin’ a certain fish is about the right bait, the right place, and luck! I got a hankerin’ for some nice, fresh striped sea bass. They’re ocean fish, so ya gotta go to the beach. They might go for a worm, but you’ll have better luck with this. *\[Gives 3x [Chub](Chub.md)\]* In the future ya can keep baitfish ya catch to try and hook bigger and more valuable fish. Come back when ya’ve caught one!

#### Part 2

Bruno has asked you to catch a sea bass. You can find them at the beach.

**Bruno:** Oh, would you look at that beauty! What would ya like for your prize?

**Tara:** I would like a shower.

**Bruno:** Nah, smellin’ like cutbait is part of the life! You’ll get used to it. Speakin’ of bait, here’s some more! *\[Gives 3x [Worm](Worm.md), 3x [Cricket](Cricket.md), 3x [Chub](Chub.md)\]* I’ve given ya three different kinds of bait. Give ‘em all a try. They’ll catch different fish in different water. Happy anglin’!
