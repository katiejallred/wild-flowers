## Description

A fried doughnut dusted with sugar and cinnamon. Can be sold to Angus.

Contrary to internet responses, the recipe for this can Not be found in the dirt piles in the mountain pass area. Only by [Fishing](Fishing.md) in the [Ocean](Ocean.md)
