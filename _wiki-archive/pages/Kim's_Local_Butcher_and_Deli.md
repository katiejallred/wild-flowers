**Kim's Local Butcher and Deli** is a small shop run by [Kim Izumi](Kim_Izumi.md). They buy and sell meat products, and will buy adult animals in exchange for coins and resources. Kim pays more than [Marty](The_Hayseed_Homestead.md) when buying [chickens](Chickens.md), and a bit less than Marty when buying [cows](Cows.md) or [sheep](Sheep.md).
