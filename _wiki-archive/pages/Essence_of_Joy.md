## Description

A heavenly-looking substance made from [complifish](Complifish.md). Used as a potion ingredient. (You can find complifish in the lake in the Gloaming.)

## Quests

Essence of Joy is used in the following [quests](Quests.md):

- A [bulletin](Bulletin_Board_Quests.md) posted by [Zephyr Grimspark](Zephyr_Grimspark.md) calls for 3 Essence of Joy
