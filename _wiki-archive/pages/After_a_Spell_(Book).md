**After a Spell (Book)** is a [quest](To-Do_Quests.md) given by [Violet Miranda](Violet_Miranda.md). It requires you to deliver 20x [Paper](Paper.md) to her so she can continue to work on replicating her mother's book. This quest is also Violet's 4-heart event.

## Dialogue

Quest intro:

- Tara: Hi Violet. Picking wildflowers?
- Violet: No, I just come here to read a book sometimes. I’m working on my own book, but I keep running out of paper since I have to use big sheets of it to wrap flowers! Do you have any to spare?

Quest completion:

- Tara: Here’s your paper, Violet.
- Violet: Thanks! This might sound like a random question but… do you ever have a hard time letting go of the past?
- --\[Choice: Who says we have to?\]
  - Tara: Of course. But I don’t know that we have to let go of the past. It made us who we are.
  - Violet: That’s true. Thanks for saying that.
- --\[Choice: No, but my dad did\]
  - \-
- --\[Choice: You can’t change the past\]
  - \-
- Violet: So this book I’ve been working on… it was my mom’s. I lost it, and I’m trying to replace it, once page at a time. But the more time passes, the harder it is for me to remember the pages. I’m worried one day I’ll wake up having forgotten it all forever.
- Tara: Let me know if I can help.
- Violet: Thanks. You’ve already helped enough.
