**Only Have Fries For You** is a [quest](To-Do_Quests.md) given by [Damon Mthembu-Haas](Damon_Mthembu-Haas.md). It requires you to deliver 3x [Potato](Potato.md) to him so he can make fries for a happy hour promotion. The reward for completion is 2x [Wine](Wine.md).

## Dialogue

Quest intro:

- Damon: Ag, man! How and I supposed to pay these new fees?
- Tara: What’s wrong, Damon?
- Damon: Mayor Soft-on-Business just signed a new rule saying I’ve got to pay for a new license just to keep my bar open! Ugh, and he only have me a few weeks to pay up!
- Tara: That doesn’t seem very fair.
- Damon: I’m not giving up the bar! I promised my ma and pa I would keep it going when they left, and I have! I know! I’ll have a happy hour promotion! Free order of fries for every customer who comes in between six and eight! Except… I don’t have any potatoes. Hey, do you have some?
- --\[Choice: Sure\]
- Tara: I could grow you some.
- Damon: Really? That would be awesome! People love free chow, this place is gonna be lit!
- --\[Choice: No\]

Quest completion:

- Damon: Hey, got my potatoes yet?
- Tara: Yes, here you go!
- Damon: Thanks! I’m gonna make slap chips the back home way my pa does, with tomato sauce and vinegar! Here, take this for helping me out!
- \[Reward: 2x Wine\]
