## Description

A refreshing zero-proof strawberry and mint drink. Can be bought and sold at [Sophia's Diner](Cafe_Moretti.md).  
  
Note: While the item description in game states that this can be bought/sold at the diner after it is unlocked, this is bought and sold at [Damon's Bar](The_Bar.md), not Sophia's Diner.
