*"Bouquets and seeds for your every need."*

  

**Violet's Violets** is a flower shop run by [Violet Miranda](Violet_Miranda.md), where she sells flower seeds. Violet also buys [honey](Honey.md) harvested from [bee hives](Bee_Hive.md), flower crops grown in [garden beds](Garden_Bed.md), and bouquets crafted at the [Bouquet Table](Bouquet_Table.md).

The shop is renamed ["Violet's Boutique"](Violet's_Boutique.md) when Violet begins selling designs for [outfits](Outfits.md) that can be crafted with the [Sewing Machine](Sewing_Machine.md).
