## Description

This magic mushroom is only found in the [Gloaming](Gloaming.md), and can be used in powerful spells.

## Information

The Trompette de la Mort can be found to the West on the first level of the Gloaming, inside a Fey Circle. Use a [Shrinking Potion](Shrinking_Potion.md) to shrink down and enter the Fey Circle, collect the mushrooms there, and use a [Stretching Potion](Stretching_Potion.md) to return to normal size.
