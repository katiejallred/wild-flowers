**The Fairhaven Coven** is a coven of witches found in [Fairhaven](Fairhaven.md).

## The Fairhaven Coven

Tara works to join the ranks of five masked witches and [Gramma](Hazel_Wylde.md) Hazel Wylde who make up the Fairhaven coven.

At first, the masked witches go by the titles of "High Priestess", "Farseer", "Journeyman", "Adept", and "Acolyte". As you complete more quests for the group, you have the task of revealing their true identities. There are multiple [To-Do Quests](To-Do_Quests.md) in which the player is tasked with figuring out who each masked member is.
