# Farming

*Categories: Farming*

*Source: <https://wylde-flowers.fandom.com/wiki/Farming>*

---

**Farming** is a game mechanic that allows the player to plant and grow crops in [Garden Beds](Garden_Bed.md) placed in the [Garden](Garden.md), Vegetable Patch and [Greenhouse](Greenhouse.md).

## Crops

Profitability is calculated by sell price (for a full crop) minus seed price divided by grow time. For fruit trees, it is slightly diferent: Their seeds are more expensive but need to be bought only once and then keep producing fruit. They also don't need to be watered. They will take a longer time to grow into maturity and then produce fruit regularly. For the calculation, the initial growing time has been ignored and the seed price lowered to 10 coins to bring it in line with other seeds.

Profitability does NOT take into consideration the earning from any products that could be crafted/cooked from these crops.

Flowers have the added benefit that they produce wax and honey in a near-by bee hive as long as they are in flower and not picked.
