Bruno Soft (72) is [Otto](Otto.md)'s dad and the owner of [Soft and Son's Fish & Tackle](Soft_and_Son's_Fish_&_Tackle.md). He's a crusty old man of the sea. Also, he likes going to the bar. A lot.

## Life Before Fairhaven

Bruno's father moved to Fairhaven from Germany when Bruno was just a baby, and Fairhaven is the only home he's known. He spent many years happily married to Helena, but has felt unmoored since Helena passed away.

## Relationship Cutscenes

**New Acquaintances** *(expand)*

**How to initiate cutscene**: After gaining one full heart, meet Bruno on Sunday at the beach below the lighthouse.

Bruno is seen fishing on the beach and states that he's looking for [flounder](Flounder.md) to make a [Fried Flounder Sandwich](Fried_Flounder_Sandwich.md), his late wife Helena's favorite. He tells Tara that he was born in Dresden but that his da fled Germany when he was young, and so he was raised fully in Fairhaven. He reveals that his mother was killed in a bombing before he and his da fled Germany. He expresses uncertainty about staying in Fairhaven since [Otto](Otto.md) is "all he \[has\]" but hardly has time for him. He concludes, "... maybe nowhere is my home these days."  
  

Dialogue:  
Tara: Heya, Bruno. Trying to catch anything in particular today?  
Bruno: How’d you know? I’m anglin’ for a few flounder. Fried flounder makes the best fish sandwich. It was my Helena’s favorite lunch!  
--\[Choice: Delicious!\]  
-Tara: Mmm, that sounds so good. Let me know if you have extra!  
-Bruno: I will, I will. Though I'm not the best at cookin' the thing.  
--\[Choice: Good luck!\]  
-Tara: I hope you catch the perfect flounder, then!  
-Bruno: This here’s the best flounder spot on the island, so my chances are good.  
--\[Choice: You don’t get bored?\]  
-  
Bruno: When I’m feelin’ a bit lonesome, I rustle up my own fish to make this sandwich. Reminds me of when Otto was a boy, and Helena was still here, and life was just the three of us…  
Tara: So you’re Fairhaven born and raised?  
Bruno: Raised, yes. But I was born in Dresden. My da fled Germany when I was real little.  
Tara: Do you remember Germany?  
Bruno: All I remember is my da tellin’ me what his life used to be like before the bombin’. That was what took my ma. I always thought Fairhaven was my only home, but now that Otto’s all I have, and he hardly has time for me… … maybe nowhere is my home these days.  
Tara: Fairhaven is still your home, Bruno. Even if Otto is distracted. I haven’t been here long, but even I know your fishing shack is an island institution!  

Bruno: Weeelll, I am real proud of that. Thanks for the kind words, Tara. I sure am glad you’re here.

  

**Casual Friend** *(expand)*

**How to initiate cutscene**:  
  
***Note:** When the game was initially released, players could only reach **one heart** with Bruno. This was later increased to **three hearts** in an update that extended the story beyond Year 1. As a result, **the Casual Friend cutscene will not trigger during Year One.** Additionally, though Bruno only has three hearts, he has four cutscenes - one at one full heart, then two for two hearts and one for three hearts.*  

After having one full heart sometime after Year One, Bruno will be waiting for you outside your house. He will invite you to go fishing with him tomorrow. Meet him at the bridge to the lighthouse the next morning.

Bruno is waiting for you outside your house. He invites you to go fishing with him the next day.
