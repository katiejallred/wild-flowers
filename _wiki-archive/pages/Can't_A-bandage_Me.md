**Can't A-bandage Me** is a [quest](To-Do_Quests.md) given by [Amira Syed](Amira_Syed.md) during the [Summer](Summer.md) after completing the quest [Out of Whole Cloth](Out_of_Whole_Cloth.md). It requires you to deliver 4x [Cotton](Cotton.md) to her. The reward for completion is 228 coins.

## Dialogue

Quest intro:

- Amira: Oh, I’m so glad to see you! I always am, of course! But I could really use your help. My clinic is out of cotton – again! Any change you’re growing any cotton on your farm? Four bunches should keep me stocked up.
- Tara: Sure, but it might take a few days!
- Amira: Thanks!

Quest completion:

- Amira: Do you have the cotton yet?
- Tara: Yes, here you go.
- Amira: Thank you so much! I can let the kids have their bikes and skateboards back now.
- Tara: Wait, you actually did confiscate them? They listened to you?
- Amira: People always do what I tell them to do. Is that strange? Anyway, here’s something for your efforts! Thanks!
- \[Reward: 228 coins\]
