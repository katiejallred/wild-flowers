# Town Hall

*Categories: Buildings, Locations*

*Source: <https://wylde-flowers.fandom.com/wiki/Town_Hall>*

---

| Field                            | Value                                        |
|----------------------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| **Official Fairhaven Town Hall** |                                              |
| Location                         | Town Center                                  |
| Inhabitants                      | Mayor Otto (Year 1), Gloria Miranda (Year 2) |

The town hall is a light brown building with white decorations, leading to a large archway. On the foundation, there are stone steps and a black sign with a bell. Next to the building, there is an errand board and on the other side, a bench.

When you enter the building, the first thing you see is the mayor's desk. This room is also a museum where the achievements of the city are presented.

Meetings are held in another room. There are chairs for participants and a stage where city events are held.
