## Description

Delicious, nutritious food for cows and sheep. Buy it from Marty.

## Notes

[Cows](Cows.md), [sheep](Sheep.md), [pigs](Pig.md), and [alpaca](Alpaca.md) each eat 1 hay per day and will fall ill if feedings are missed for multiple days. Until building the [Hay Field](Hay_Field.md) on your own farm, hay must be purchased periodically from [Marty](Marty.md). The hay field does not produce hay as a item, but does take care of feeding all of your barn animals without it!
