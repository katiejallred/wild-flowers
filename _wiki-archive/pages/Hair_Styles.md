# Hair Styles

*Source: <https://wylde-flowers.fandom.com/wiki/Hair_Styles>*

---

Hair Styles can be purchased from [Coiffed & Crowned by Eury](Coiffed_&_Crowned_by_Eury.md) using coins, hair color, and pomade. The hair salon is unlocked in Summer Y2. Once a hairstyle is purchased, you can select it at any time via the hair salon or your inventory.

Hair color and pomade can be crafted at the [Mortar and Pestle](Mortar_and_Pestle.md) in your sanctum.
