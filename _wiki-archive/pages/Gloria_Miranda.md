Gloria Miranda (51) is the mother of [Violet](Violet.md) and [Sebastián](Sebastián.md). She disappeared 4 years ago. She used to be the First Lady of Fairhaven when her husband, Antonio, was mayor.  

**Click to reveal spoilers!**

Gloria is a powerful witch recently discovered to be alive, years after mysteriously vanishing the Gloaming.

## Life Before Fairhaven

Before her disappearance, she led a double life as both the First Lady of Fairhaven - her husband, Antonio, was mayor - and as a powerful witch.  
**Click to reveal spoilers!**

After Gloria tried to stop a wraith, she was trapped in the Gloaming for years. Now that you've freed her, she's been reunited with her children, [Violet](Violet.md) and [Sebastián](Sebastián.md).

## Other Information

Gloria works at Town Hall M-F from 9 AM to 5 PM.

After work, Gloria is found on the bench outside Town Hall until she leaves for the Coven meeting.

On Saturdays, Gloria can be found on the bench outside the doctor’s office and near the Willow Tree in the forest. On Sundays, Gloria can be found at the Willow Tree in the forest.
