The Sewing Machine has four categories of outfits that can be made: Casual, Work, Formal, and Magic.  
  
-Casual, Work, & Formal Outfit designs are purchased in [Violet's Boutique](Violet's_Boutique.md); new patterns are stocked as Tara sells outfits to Violet.  
-Magic Outfit designs are first unlocked after reading a letter from Violet after collecting magical animal produce for the first time.

**Base Materials**

  
  
**Dye/Other**  
Dyes can be crafted with a [mortar and pestle](Mortar_and_Pestle.md).  
Dyed cloth/silk/leather/thread can be achieved via a [dyeing bath](Dyeing_Bath.md) using the corresponding dye.  
Coatings can be crafted with a [cauldron](Cauldron.md).  
Magic Cloth can be crafted with a [loom](Loom.md) using corresponding magical animal wool.
