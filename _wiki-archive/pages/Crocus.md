## Description

A purple, white, or yellow flower that grows in [Winter](Winter.md). Use it as a crafting ingredient or sell it to [Violet](Violet_Miranda.md).

## Other information

Crocus crops that are fully grown but not harvested contribute to any nearby [Bee Hive](Bee_Hive.md)'s production of [Honey](Honey.md).
