# Bulletin Board Quests

*Categories: Quests*

*Source: <https://wylde-flowers.fandom.com/wiki/Bulletin_Board_Quests>*

---

**Bulletin Board** quests are available from the start of the game by interacting with the board just to the left of the town hall entrance. Another board is available in [Ravenwood Hollow](Ravenwood_Hollow.md) later in the game. One new request is posted at the start of each day, and up to 4 may be available at any time.

Requests are posted by villagers asking you to turn in some number of a single item, which is rewarded with coins and an increase in friendship level. There is no time limit for completing the quests and no penalty for deleting the requests from the board or your journal.
