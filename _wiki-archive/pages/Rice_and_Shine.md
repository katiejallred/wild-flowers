**Rice and Shine** is a [quest](To-Do_Quests.md) given by [Gramma](Hazel_Wylde.md). It requires you to collect 2x [Mushroom](Mushroom.md) and buy 1x [Rice](Rice.md) in order to cook [Mushroom Risotto](Mushroom_Risotto.md).

## Dialogue

Quest intro:

- Gramma: See? A bit of food is all you need when your energy is low and then you’re right as rain for a while. You’ll still need plenty of sleep – even *my* cooking is no match for a good night’s rest.
- Tara: But Gramma, you’re the one who should be resting! I don’t want you troubling yourself to cook for me!
- Gramma: Ah, is that your way of trying to get my recipe book? Did Sophia put you up to it? She’s always trying to get a peek at my recipes – but you can tell her they stay in the family! I hide my recipe book in the stove. She never thinks to look in there, haha! Go on, try one!
- Tara: Okay! Well, which one is your favorite?
- Gramma: I am quite partial to Mushroom Risotto!

Part 1: Buy rice and collect mushrooms so you can make Gramma’s favorite dish.

- Tara: I think I have everything we need.
- Gramma: Now, I’ve written down the recipe here. Head on over to the stove and give it a try!

Part 2: Use the stove to cook [Mushroom Risotto](Mushroom_Risotto.md).

- Tara: Here you go, Gramma. Here’s your mushroom risotto!
- Gramma: Oh, thank you, honeybee. It smells wonderful! But you made enough for yourself, I hope?
