*"Step in for some pie and other classic comfort foods"*

  

Cafe Moretti is run by [Sophia Moretti](Sophia_Moretti.md) and sells foods that can restore a substantial amount of energy. Several villagers are known to frequent the cafe at different times of day, and selling cooked dishes to Sophia can be a great source of profit!
