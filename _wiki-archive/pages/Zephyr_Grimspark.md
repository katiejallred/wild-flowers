Zephyr Grimspark (40) is a goblin and the resident mechanic. She's inquisitive, helpful, and can fix just about anything... except Fairhaven's weather.

## Life Before Fairhaven

Zephyr's ancestors used to live on Fairhaven's shores, but they created [Ravenwood Hollow](Ravenwood_Hollow.md) when the climate became too unstable. Zephyr has never lived outside of [Ravenwood Hollow](Ravenwood_Hollow.md), and she'd like to visit other goblin towns one day.
