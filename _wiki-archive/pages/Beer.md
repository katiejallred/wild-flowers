## Description

Beer can be made by fermenting hops in a [Fermentation Barrel](Fermentation_Barrel.md) in your tool shed. Drinking it replenishes magic but costs energy.  
  

## Favorite Gift

Beer is one of [Bruno](Bruno.md)'s favorite gifts.
