# Farm

*Categories: Areas*

*Source: <https://wylde-flowers.fandom.com/wiki/Farm>*

---

![Wylde Farm](../images/Wylde_Farm.jpg)

**The Farm** serves as Tara's home base throughout the game and supports crafting in the [kitchen](Cooking.md), [Basement](Basement.md), and [Tool Shed](Tool_Shed.md), farming in the [Garden](Garden.md) and additional farm plot, and animal rearing in the [Barn](Barn.md) and [chicken coop](Chickens.md). There are resources to forage along the left and lower parts of the area, as well as respawning trees to chop for [wood](Wood.md) and rocks to break for [stone](Stone.md). Early on raw [wood](Wood.md) may occasionally spawn on the ground, serving as the only source of this resource before obtaining a suitable [wood axe](Blacksmith.md)!

## Connections

- [Town Center](Town_Center.md) (upper right; exits next to [Blacksmith](Blacksmith.md))
- [Beach](Beach.md) (bottom left; exits north of [Thomas](Thomas.md)'s farm)
- [Mountain Overpass](Mountain_Overpass.md) (top left, once unlocked)
- [Mine](Mine.md) (following the path along the left side of screen, once unlocked)
- [Ravenwood Hollow](Ravenwood_Hollow.md) (mid-left, once unlocked)
