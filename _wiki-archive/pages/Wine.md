## Description

Drinking wine replenishes some magic but costs some energy. Wine can be made in a [fermentation barrel](Fermentation_barrel.md) in the [tool shed](Tool_shed.md), and it can be sold to Damon.  
  

## Favorite Gift

Wine is one of [Westley](Westley.md)'s favorite gifts.
