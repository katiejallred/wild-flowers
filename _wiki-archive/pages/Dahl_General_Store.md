*"We buy and sell seeds, vegetables and kitchen supplies. Continuously in operation since the 1800s!"*

  

**Dahl General Store** is a store run by [Lina Dahl-Johnson](Lina_Dahl-Johnson.md), located in the center of the town square. It stocks a variety of seeds, produce, and other food items, and is the primary location at which Tara can sell the crops she has grown.
