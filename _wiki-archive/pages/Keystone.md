# Keystone

*Categories: Wylde Flowers*

*Source: <https://wylde-flowers.fandom.com/wiki/Keystone>*

---

**Keystones** are circular rocks with symbols on them that are used at the standing stones in order to travel into [Gloaming](Gloaming.md). There are three keystones in total, each one leading to a different part of the Gloaming.

The first keystone is acquired from the display case in Town Hall during the quest [At the Break-in Point](At_the_Break-in_Point.md).

The second keystone is found behind a magical barrier in the first section of the Gloaming.

The third keystone is obtained along with the [Adder Stone](Adder_Stone.md).
