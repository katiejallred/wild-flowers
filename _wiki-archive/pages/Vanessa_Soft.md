Vanessa Soft (29) is the only teacher at Fairhaven's only school. As [Otto](Otto.md)'s wife, she's the First Lady of Fairhaven, a position suited to her polished, sophisticated persona.

## Life Before Fairhaven

When Vanessa was a child, her family used to spend summers in Fairhaven. She developed a strong love for the island that persists to this day.

## Relationship Cutscenes

**Friends** *(expand)*

**How to initiate cutscene**: After gaining one full heart, find Vanessa at the southern part of the lake in the Woods on the weekend.

Dialogue:  

Tara: Vanessa? Is that you?  
Vanessa: Oh! Hello, Tara. I was just admiring the water. It looks shallow, but it’s surprisingly deep here. It makes for a lovely swimming hole.  
Tara: Does Otto know that you go swimming in the lake that’s, uh, inside the woods he absolutely detests?  
Vanessa: I haven’t swum here in years. But when I was a little girl, my family came here on vacation every summer. Those weeks were always the best part of the year. They were how I knew Fairhaven was special to me. You visited as well when you were a child… did you also have that sense?  
--\[Choice: Yes\]  
-Tara: Yes! Whenever my dad brought me to visit my grandmother, I didn’t want to go back.  
-Vanessa: Neither did I! I wonder what our lives would have been like if we’d grown up here?  
--\[Choice: It was complicated\]  
-Tara: I always wanted to come back, but my dad and grandmother... well, I didn't even know why they were fighting.  
-Vanessa: I understand. My parents pushed me very hard when I was a child. It took me a long time to understand why they did it. They just wanted me to succeed.  
--\[Choice: No\]  
-  
Vanessa: In any case… I love Fairhaven, even the parts of it my husband doesn’t care for. Sometimes it’s hard for me to express that love. But I do feel it.  
Tara: That’s really sweet, Vanessa.  
Vanessa: Well, I mean it! Oh, and Tara? Don’t tell everyone about my spot. I like having space to just be.
