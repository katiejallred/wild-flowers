[to](Potato.md) to him. The reward for completion is 2x [Croissant](Croissant.md) and 100 coins.

## Dialogue

Quest intro:

- Angus: How lucky you’ve stopped in! I’ve got an awesome Seton family mushroom pierogi recipe just waiting for potatoes and mushrooms!
- Tara: Um, I thought you were Scottish, not Polish?
- Angus: Mom’s side is from Poland! Although we did try filling them with haggis once, which I would…. Not recommend. If you can help me out, I’ll give you a cut of the sale price and some carb-loaded goodness to take home!

Quest completion:

- Angus: Oooh, is that what I think it is?
- Tara: Yep, here’s your potatoes and mushrooms.
- Angus: Yay! These pierogies are gonna be so awesome! Here’s your cut and some croissants, you must be starving!
- \[Reward: 100 coins, 2 croissants\]
