From Bud To Spud is a [quest](To-Do_Quests.md) given by [Gramma](Hazel_Wylde.md). It is a tutorial quest that teaches you to plant, water, harvest, and sell crops. It begins immediately on completion of [To Gramma's House I Go](To_Gramma's_House_I_Go.md), and the player cannot leave the farm area without planting and watering the potato seeds.

## Dialogue

Quest intro:

- [Gramma](Hazel_Wylde.md): Blasted rusty old crank! I oughta blast you! Heh. Not a bad idea, actually. My charm will bind, turn, and unwind. Hehe! Look at it go!
- [Tara](Tara_Wylde.md): Uh… Gramma?
- [Gramma](Hazel_Wylde.md): Oh! It’s you, my little honey bee!
- [Tara:](Tara_Wylde.md) I’m not so little anymore, Gramma.
- [Gramma](Hazel_Wylde.md): No, I suppose not! I’m so glad you’ve come.
- [Tara](Tara_Wylde.md): Me too. How can I help out ‘round here? I don’t know much about farming, but maybe you could show me the ropes!
- [Gramma](Hazel_Wylde.md): Err… well… now that you mention it… I haven’t got the spring crop of potatoes in yet.
- [Tara](Tara_Wylde.md): Let me help, Gramma. Why don’t you give me those seeds and I can get them planted.
- [Gramma](Hazel_Wylde.md): That would be a good start! Here’s the seeds. \[Gives potato seeds.\] Just head over to one of the garden beds and scatter them about.

Part 1: Plant the [Potato Seeds](Potato_Seeds.md) in the garden bed by the farm.

- [Gramma](Hazel_Wylde.md): You’re a natural! Now you’ll need to water them if you want them to grow. \[Gives rusty watering can.\] You can fill it up at this old well.

Part 2: Plants need to be watered every day to grow. Fill up the Watering Can at the Well.

- [Gramma](Hazel_Wylde.md): There now. That should do it! They’ll take a few days to grow, but it’s a start!

Part 3: Wait for your [Potato Seeds](Potato_Seeds.md) to produce [Potatoes](Potato.md).  
**Hint:** Water the potato plant every day. Mature potatoes will be ready in a few days.

- [Gramma](Hazel_Wylde.md): I can’t thank you enough for helping out here, sweetheart. Are the potatoes ready to harvest?
  - --\[Choice: Not yet\]
- [Tara](Tara_Wylde.md): No, Gramma. They’re not ready quite yet.
- [Gramma](Hazel_Wylde.md): Oh. Well, that’s okay. Nature takes time, you can’t rush her!
  - --\[Choice: Yes, here they are\]
- [Tara](Tara_Wylde.md): Yes, Gramma, here’s 4 potatoes from the field.
- [Gramma](Hazel_Wylde.md): Wonderful! Maybe you’ve got a greener thumb than you thought! Why don’t you take them down to the General Store and sell them? I’m sure Lina will give us a fair price for them.

Part 4: Go to the [general store](Dahl_General_Store.md) and sell the potatoes to Lina.

- [Tara](Tara_Wylde.md): Lina bought the potatoes just as you said. Here’s the money, Gramma.
- [Gramma](Hazel_Wylde.md): I won’t hear of it. You did all the work, you deserve the reward.
- [Tara](Tara_Wylde.md): But Gramma, I-
- [Gramma](Hazel_Wylde.md): I mean it! If you want to keep fixing things up around here, you’ll need all the cash you can get anyway. Buy some more seeds. If we keep the fields planted, we’ll always have a way to make ends meet.
- [Tara](Tara_Wylde.md): Well… okay, but only because I’m going to use the earnings to help the farm.

Immediately begins quest **[The Fungus Among Us](The_Fungus_Among_Us.md)** and **[The Most Elaborate Icebreaker](The_Most_Elaborate_Icebreaker.md)**.  
Time will un-pause - 10am (Monday).
