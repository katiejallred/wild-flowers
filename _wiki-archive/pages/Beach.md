# Beach

*Categories: Areas*

*Source: <https://wylde-flowers.fandom.com/wiki/Beach>*

---

![Screenshot of the "Oceanview Countryside" location](../images/Oceanview_Countyside.jpg)

**The Beach** consists of two upper and two lower areas. The upper left and right areas are separated by a wooden bridge, with each featuring a slope down to the lower area on its same side. There is no bridge connecting the lower left and right areas directly, instead requiring that you travel up and across to get to one from the other.

## Connections

- [Farm](Farm.md) (upper left; exits to the Farm's southwest)
- [Town Center](Town_Center.md) (upper right; exits next to [Kim's Local Butcher and Deli](Kim's_Local_Butcher_and_Deli.md) & lower right; exits next to [The Bar](The_Bar.md))
