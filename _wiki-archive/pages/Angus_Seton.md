Angus Seton (28) is Fairhaven's baker and Francis's fiancé.  
  
His puns are as bad as his cakes are delicious, and he radiates sunny positivity, except when he worries about his relationship with Francis. Ultimately, though, he can't wait to get married.  
  
He joined Cameron's Consciousness Center, which he sees as a place of solace and strength.  
  
Angus sees witchcraft as a threat, and sightings of cloaked people at night have made him fearful and protective.

## Life Before Fairhaven

Angus is the middle of three brothers, which sometimes made him feel overlooked.

## Relationship Cutscenes

**New Acquaintances** *(expand)*

**How to initiate cutscene**: After gaining one full heart, visit Angus at [Bakery](Seton's_Bakery.md).

Dialogue:  

Tara: Oooh, baking something new, Angus?  
Angus: Yep! It’s my little bro’s favorite! I’m the middle child of three boys, so it was nonstop chaos growing up. But I could always get Jamie to calm down if I gave him one of these! You don’t have any siblings, do you? That must have been a bit lonely growing up, no?  
--\[Choice: Sometimes\]  
-  
--\[Choice: Not really\]  
-Tara: Not when I was little, because I spent my summers here and grew close to my grandma and grandpa.  
-Angus: Well, of course. Having a grandma like Hazel must have been so much fun!  
--\[Choice: Yes\]  
-Tara: It was. I want to have a big family of my own someday.  
-Angus: Hey, big families have their downsides too, believe me!  
Angus: Francis and I are both the middle child of three-child families. Maybe that’s why we’re perfect for each other – we’re used to being the overlooked cheese in the sandwich! I hope Francis is able to find a place for himself here and that everyone sees just how special he truly is.

  

  

**Casual Friend** *(expand)*

**How to initiate cutscene**: After gaining two full hearts, visit [League of the Conscious Mind](League_of_the_Conscious_Mind.md) on a Sunday.

Dialogue:  

Angus: Hi Tara! Good to see you here! Do you come often?  
Tara: Um… not really!  
Angus: That’s okay! Everyone has to walk the path at their own pace, as Messenger Cameron says! I grew up going to Mass, so it took me a while to feel comfortable here knowing my parents wouldn’t approve. And Francis’s snarky remarks don’t help.  
--\[Choice: He should respect you\]  
-Tara: Well, hopefully Francis will learn to respect your beliefs even if he doesn’t share them.  
-Angus: I hope so. It’s not like he’s against this specific set of beliefs, it’s more like religion in general? I think it has to do with his family, but I don’t pry because he shuts down when I ask about it.  
--\[Choice: Don’t worry about it\]  
-Tara: Hey, you're entitled to your beliefs. Don't worry about what others think.  
-Angus: Don't worry about what others think? Hello, have you even met me? I can't help it! But you're right. I shouldn't care so much.  
--\[Choice: It is unusual\]  
-Tara: They'll come around, but I can see why your family and Francis might find the League a bit unusual.  
-Angus: It definitely is new! But they shouldn't be so quick to judge before they've even given it a chance! I think the Book of the Mind can really help people. Angus: Look, I feel real acceptance here. Like I can be in charge of my own destiny, you know? It’s about hope.

  

  

**Good Friend** *(expand)*

**How to initiate cutscene**: After gaining three full hearts, visit [flower shop](Violet's_Violets.md) on Wednesday.

Note: this must be completed before Angus and Francis get married.

Dialogue:  

Violet: Unfortunately, Angus, my local supplier is all out of peonies right now. I could get them from the hothouse importer, but they’ll be really expensive. What about roses?  
Angus: Oh no, Francis only wants peonies for the ceremony! I prefer roses, to be honest. They smell good! But Francis will find them too pedestrian. What do you think, Tara?  
--\[Choice: Get the roses\]  
-Tara: Just get the roses, Angus! They're classic! Francis does have good taste, but it's your wedding too!  
-Angus: You're right! The wedding should be a reflection of both of us! We're getting the roses!  
--\[Choice: Get wildflowers\]  
-Tara: What about wildflowers? They would be the cheapest option, and they’re definitely not typical.  
-Angus: I love it! A local, authentic touch! Francis can’t argue with that!  
--\[Choice: Get the peonies\]  
-Tara: I think you should give Francis what he wants. He does have really good taste.  
-Angus: I know he does... but sometimes the rest of us would like to have an opinion, you know?  
Violet: Okay! I’ll get you an estimate soon! It’s going to look so romantic!  
Angus: Yay! I’m getting so excited I just can’t wait to get married already! Thanks for your imput, Tara!

  

  

**Best Friend** *(expand)*

**How to initiate cutscene**: After gaining four full hearts, visit Angus at [Bakery](Seton's_Bakery.md).

Dialogue:  

Angus: Oh. Hey there, Tara. Don’t mind me, I’m just bumming a bit today. My baby brother was supposed to visit but he got slammed with work and couldn’t come. It’s probably for the best.  
Tara: Why would you say that? I know how close you guys are.  
Angus: Yeah, but it doesn’t really feel safe here. Otto says people wearing cloaks are going into the woods at night. I don’t know what they’re up to, but it can’t be anything good.  
--\[Choice: The town is just quirky\]  
-  
--\[Choice: We shouldn’t judge them\]  
-Tara: Whomever the people in the woods are, they might not mean any harm! We shouldn't rush to judge them anymore than you would want to be judged for your beliefs.  
-Angus: I guess that's a fair point. But you have no idea how dangerous witches can be, Tara. It's not all superstition. They hurt people.  
--\[Choice: I haven’t seen anything\]  
-Tara: I don’t know, I haven’t seen anything all that strange.  
-Angus: You can’t be serious. You’ve never seen the people in cloaks?  
Angus: I’m not the only one who thinks something is wrong, Tara. Thing is, I’ve always felt really protective of Jamie. So long as he’s safe, that’s all that matters to me.  
Tara: You’re safe too, Angus. This is a safe place.  

Angus: It’s kind of you to reassure me. But I think we all have to be on the lookout.
