**Aryel** is a <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curupira" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Curupira">Curupira demon</a> who runs [Ravenwood Hollow](Ravenwood_Hollow.md)'s menagerie. He's playful, but also fiercely protective of the forest and the animals he rehabilitates in his menagerie.

## Life Before Fairhaven

Aryel once lived on the banks of the Amazon River, where he tricked poachers into staying away from local wildlife. After getting trapped on a poacher's boat, he escaped and found himself near an unfamiliar island: Fairhaven.

## Relationship Cutscenes

**Friends** *(expand)*

**How to initiate cutscene**: After gaining one full heart, speak to Aryel in his [shop](Pig's_Wings_and_Dragonlings.md).

Dialogue:  

Aryel: Shriek!  
Tara: Geez, Aryel. Long day or something?  
Aryel: Aiiiiieeee! Tara! My apologies. I didn’t realize you were here. I am practicing my diabolical sounds! Other fae don’t hear my cries but humans are driven mad by them. … eventually. You’re in no danger.  
Tara: And do these sounds have a purpose? Or are you just letting off steam?  
Aryel: Why, of course they do! When I shriek, I drive away humans and keep the land safe for those with fins and feathers.  
--\[Choice: That sounds useful\]  
-Tara: That’s a useful way to keep humans from going where they’re not supposed to go.  
-Aryel: Oh, yes! A sensitive instrument, the human ear.  
--\[Choice: I’m sorry\]  
-Tara: I'm sorry you have to keep humans from destroying animals' habitats. That isn't fair.  
-Aryel: Is it unfair? It doesn't feel that way to me. I was born to do this.  
--\[Choice: Not everyone\]  
-  
Tara: Did anyone teach your cries to you?  
Aryel: No, no, no. I came into being knowing them. I lived on the bank of a river with many other curupiras. The other curupiras and I used our feet to lead the poachers astray. We used our voices to make the poachers think they had gone mad! It was just fabulous.  
Tara: It’s a long journey from there to here.  
Aryel: Very long. One day, I followed the poachers to their ship. I was investigating belowdecks when – slam! – the hatch came crashing down. A few days later, one of the sailors opened the hatch. I screamed and screamed! It was excellent!  
Tara: What happened then?  
Aryel: He picked me up and threw me overboard! But I swim better than any fish. I cam ashore on the nearest island, and I have never left.  
Tara: But don’t you ever want to go back? Or get revenge on those poachers?  
Aryel: Oh, I got my revenge, you can be sure of that! As for going back… maybe one day. But the animals of this island needed protection as much as the animals of my home forest.  
Tara: They’re lucky to have you, Aryel.  
Aryel: I know! I tell them so frequently!
