## Description

Earth-friendly, hand-made paper embedded with pressed wild flowers.

## Other information

Can be crafted using any kind of [Forage](Forage.md) flower found in the game, such as [Thistle](Thistle.md), [Wolfsbane](Wolfsbane.md), [Poppy Flower](Poppy_Flower.md), [Heather](Heather.md), [Witch Weed](Witch_Weed.md), [Forget-Me-Not](Forget-Me-Not.md), Mistletoe, and [Nigella](Nigella.md)
