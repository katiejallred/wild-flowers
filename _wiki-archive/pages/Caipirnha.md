## Description

A cocktail made of sugar and lime and rum. Drinking it restores some magic but costs some energy.

You cannot buy the recipe from the other villagers but you can find the recipe by fishing or digging up the dirt piles in the mountain pass behind Tara’s house  
  

## Favorite Gift

Caipirinha is one of [Aryel](Aryel.md)'s favorite gifts.
