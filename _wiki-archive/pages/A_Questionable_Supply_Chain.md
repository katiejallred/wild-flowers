**A Questionable Supply Chain** is a [quest](To-Do_Quests.md) given by [Otto Soft](Otto_Soft.md). It requires you to deliver10x [Wood](Wood.md), 10x [Stone](Stone.md), and 2x [Iron Ingot](Iron_Ingot.md) to him. The reward for completion is 150 coins.

## Dialogue

Quest intro:

- Otto: Perfect! You’re exactly who I was hoping to see today.
- Tara: Hey Mayor Soft, what’s up?
- Otto: An opportunity has presented itself for you to give back to the town!
- Tara: … yay?
- Otto: The town stores are empty and we need them refilled as quickly as possible.
- Tara: Empty? Where did they all go?
- Otto: I was… they were… That is to say, that’s not really important right now. What matters is that we put them back before anyone wants to use them and notices. Okay?
- Tara: Notices? Do you mean they were stolen?
- Otto: What?! Oh. Yes. Yes that is exactly what happened. Will you help me refill them?
- Tara: I guess? What do you need?
- Otto: Wonderful. You’ll need to get 2 Iron, 10 Wood and 10 Stone. You need to get them to me ASAP or I’ll- or we’ll all be in big trouble!

Quest completion:

- Otto: Tell me you’ve got the supplies. We’re running out of time here! Haha.
- --\[Choice: It’ll take a while\]
- Tara: There’s a lot to do. I’m still working on it.
- Otto: Well hurry! We don’t have much time.
- Tara: Sigh
- --\[Choice: Yes, it’s right here.\]
- Tara: Yes, it’s right here. Geez, take a breath and calm down.
- Otto: Well, you cut it pretty close, missy! There was no need to unsettle me so. Well then, here is your pay for handling this trifling matter.
- Tara: Thanks! So, are you going to tell me what this was all about now?
- Otto: Oh, sorry. I am not at liberty to discuss town business with you. Good day.
- \[Reward: 150 coins\]
