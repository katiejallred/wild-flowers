Euryale 'Eury' Sinclair (33), is a globe trotting hair stylist with a genuine Louisiana drawl. She's just settled into Fairhaven's new storefront to open a salon.

## Life Before Fairhaven

Eury is a hair stylist who reaches out to [Gloria](Gloria_Miranda.md) about moving to [Fairhaven](Fairhaven.md) in Summer of Year 2. After [Tara](Tara_Wylde.md) helps [Parker](Parker_Johnson.md) build a [salon](Coiffed_&_Crowned_by_Eury.md) for her, Eury moves to town.

Originally from <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Orleans" class="external text" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">New Orleans</a>, Eury loves classic New Orleans-style food and culture. She is knowledgeable about all hair types and helps all of the residents in Fairhaven with their style.

She works in the salon building where you can buy new [hairstyles](Hair_Styles.md) and sell items such as [pomade](Pomade.md), [conditioner](Conditioner.md), or [h](Enchanted_Hair_Color.md)[a](Olive_Hair_Color.md)[i](Green_Hair_Color.md)[r](Red_Hair_Color.md) [d](Blue_Hair_Color.md)[y](Orange_Hair_Color.md)[e](Blonde_Hair_Color.md)[.](Black_Hair_Color.md)

## Other Information

Eury’s salon is open Saturday - Tuesday from 9 - 5. On her days off, she is found at the [beach](Beach.md), in [Sophia’s diner](Cafe_Moretti.md), at the [doctor’s office](Dr._Syed's_General_Practice.md), in the [bakery](Seton's_Bakery.md), and visiting the [Onsen](Mountain.md).

Eury will move to Fairhaven after a scene is triggered with Gloria at Town Hall. Once you are in Summer of Year 2, enter Fairhaven from Tara’s farm on a day when the Town Hall is open (M-F, between 9 AM and 5 PM). Gloria will tell Tara that she is hoping to have a new hair stylist move to town and ask Tara to help Parker with the building materials. After visiting Parker with the materials and waiting two days, the salon will be built and Eury will move in. Visit the salon for the first time to meet Eury.
