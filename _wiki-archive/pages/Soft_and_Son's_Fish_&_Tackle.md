*"We buy and sell fish and tackle"*

  

Soft and Son's Fish & Tackle is a small stall run by [Bruno Soft](Bruno_Soft.md), located to the left of [Cafe Moretti](Cafe_Moretti.md). This is the primary source of bait in the game (and the *only* source of [chub](Chub.md) until the [Mountain Overpass](Mountain_Overpass.md) is unlocked) as Compost Bins do not supply a significant amount of [worms](Worm.md) and there is no other way to obtain [crickets](Cricket.md) at this time. The name "Soft **and Son's** Fish & Tackle" refers to Bruno's son, Mayor [Otto Soft](Otto_Soft.md), who is never seen interacting with the stand -- a source of minor contention between father and son.
