## Description

Baked chunks of fish coated in breadcrumbs. Can be sold to Sophia.  
  

## Favorite Gift

Fish Fingers is one of [Emmi](Emmi.md)'s favorite gifts.

## Notes

It is possible to earn nearly infinite money by purchasing [flour](Flour.md) from [Lina](Lina.md) for 15 coins, [Tuna](Tuna.md) from [Bruno](Bruno.md) for 20 coins, and cooking them into fish fingers which sell for 100 coins (net profit of 65 coins per item). For maximum efficiency, it is best to aim for a time when all three businesses are open (Tuesday & Wednesday 11am-5pm) and repeatedly spend all of your money on ingredients for exponential growth of profits!
