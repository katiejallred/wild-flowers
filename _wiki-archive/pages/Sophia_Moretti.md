Sophia Moretti (36) is the warm, chatty proprietor of the town diner, [Cafe Moretti](Cafe_Moretti.md), as well as Juliet's mother. She specializes in Italian Jewish dishes.

Sophia is Jewish and Italian-American, but has never visited Italy - she feels no need to travel. In contrast, her late husband Giacomo, was born in Italy. Ever since his death, she has struggled to adjust to life as a widow, and she sometimes goes to the graveyard to speak to Giacomo's grave. She's trying to keep her tiny family tight-knit, but she feels like Juliet is pushing her away.

**How to initiate cutscene**: After gaining four full hearts, visit Sophia at Cafe Moretti during her work hours.

Dialogue:

Sophia: I’ve been experimenting! I’m adding some new authentic Italian dishes to the menu. My husband was from Italy, did you know that? I never had the chance to visit his family. He and Juliet once, to meet his grandmother when she was still alive. Have you traveled much, Tara?

-Sophia: Me either. Three plane tickets anywhere was a lot! That's why only the two of them went to Italy.  
--\[Choice: Yes, I love it!\]  
-Tara: Yes, and I can't wait to go off somewhere again! I get so bored being in the same place.  
-Sophia: Really? I'm never bored here. But then, I'm more interested in people than in places.  
Sophia: After they got back from Italy, Juliet would hardly talk to Giacomo. She said something about her great-grandmother being weird, but that was all. But Giacomo’s grandmother didn’t speak one word of English! She couldn’t have said anything to offend Juliet. Teenagers!  
Tara: My dad didn’t talk to my grandpa for a long time, and he was hardly a teenager. They made up after I was born.  
Sophia: Well, at least they patched things up before Robert passed. I wish Juliet and Giacomo had that closure. Always tell those you love how you feel about them, Tara. Don’t wait.
