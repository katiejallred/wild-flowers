## Description

An extremely valuable and rare fungus only found by magical pigs. Used in recipes or sell to Kai.

## Information

White truffles are found by adult magical pigs (non-magical pigs only find regular [truffles](Truffle.md)). White truffles can be picked up by petting the pigs.
