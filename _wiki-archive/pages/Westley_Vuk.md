**Westley Vuk** (35) is a werewolf and [Ravenwood Hollow](Ravenwood_Hollow.md)'s bookseller.

## About

He's polite, dapper, and has more arcane knowledge than you thought was possible.

He arrives on the island to return Aunt Vivian's journal to you, and later visits to give you missing pages. After this, he is typically found in [Fairhaven](Fairhaven.md) on Tuesdays and Wednesdays sitting on a bench outside Damon's bar or in Kim's Butcher Shop.

Westley's passion is folklore, and he has a large collection of fairy tales from around the world.

His massive orders from Kim's butcher shop are the stuff of legend.

Once he became a werewolf, he came to Ravenwood Hollow seeking a cure for his lycanthropy, but couldn't find one. He likes the other fae, but misses living with other humans. However, his relationship with you seems to be curing that ache somewhat (Only if in a relationship with Westley).

### Background

Westley was born in Alaska and grew up in his maternal grandparents' house situated in a small logging town. He got his love for old stories from his grandmother. His father walked out of the family and returned to Russia when he was three years old.

He became a werewolf due to a case of mistaken identity. His lida twin brother, Lou, was a marksman for a logging company, and shot at a werewolf who then returned and attacked Westley one full moon. Once Westley turned into a werewolf, he vowed to keep his distance from humans to protect them and himself, and found safe harbor in the magical village of Ravenwood Hollow.

The only person in Westley's family to whom he recounted his misadventure is his brother, who unfortunately rejected him afterward.
