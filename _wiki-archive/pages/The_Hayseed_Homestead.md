**"Adorable baby animals and 100% organic feed for sale!"**

The Hayseed Homestead is unlocked after the [Ranch](Ranch.md) has been restored, [Marty Emerson](Marty_Emerson.md) has moved to town, and the [Coop](Coop.md) has been fixed up on Tara's farm. This is the only source of farm animals and feed for your animals (until the [Hay Field](Hay_Field.md) is upgraded, though [chicken feed](Chicken_feed.md) still needs to come from here).

Marty will also buy back animals, offering higher prices for happier animals, offering a slightly lower profit than [Kim's Local Butcher and Deli](Kim's_Local_Butcher_and_Deli.md) for [chickens](Chickens.md) and slightly *higher* for [cows](Cows.md) and [sheep](Sheep.md) -- unlike [Kim](Kim.md), however, Marty will also buy back animals before they've fully matured.

**Before Ranch is Restored:**  
**Tara:** Look at this old run-down ranch. I hope it gets fixed up one day.
