**A Corny Request** is a [quest](To-Do_Quests.md) given by [Thomas Lightfoot](Thomas_Lightfoot.md) in the [Summer](Summer.md) after completing the request [This Party is Lit](This_Party_is_Lit.md). It requires you to grow 5x [Corn](Corn.md) and deliver it to [Thomas](Thomas_Lightfoot.md) so he can make popcorn for the Summer Festival. The reward for completion is 3x [Caramel Popcorn](Caramel_Popcorn.md).

## Dialogue

Quest intro:

- Tara: It’s really nice of you to help Sophia out.
- Thomas: If that’s bait, washashore, I’m not bitin’.
- Tara: Well, nice talking to ya, Thomas!
- Thomas: Wait. I…. ugh… need a favor.
- Tara: How can I help?
- Thomas: The Summer Festival is meant to celebrate the bounty of Fairhaven. Sophia and Angus spend the whole week leading up to it cooking and baking. Sophia asked me if I’d help her with the corn for popcorn. I’ve filled up as many of my plots as I can spare, but it won’t be enough.
- Tara: You need me to grow corn?
- Thomas: Yep. Here are the seeds. 5 bushels oughta do it. \[Gives 2x [Corn Seeds](Corn_Seeds.md).\]

Part 1: Thomas needs corn so he can make popcorn for the Summer Festival.

- Tara: Here’s the corn for Sophia, Thomas.
- Thomas: Thanks. She’s a single mom, running her business all on her own. She deserves all the help she can get.
- Tara: Uh-huh.
- Thomas: Don’t know why you’ve got that goofy grin on yer face. Just a friend helping a friend. Why, she say anything?
- Tara: It’s not about what she says, Thomas. It’s about-
- Thomas: Okay, okay! Don’t need advice from you! Anyhow, come see my on festival day.
- Tara: Sure, why?
- Thomas: Just do it.

Part 2: Thomas says you should visit him during the Summer Festival. Maybe you’ll get to taste that popcorn…?

- Thomas: Here's some popcorn. No butter, and we ran out of salt…
- Tara: It's… great. Thanks, Thomas.
- \[Reward: 3x Caramel Popcorn\]
