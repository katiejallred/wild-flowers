**Tara Wylde** is the playable character and protagonist of [Wylde Flowers](Wylde_Flowers.md).

A young woman from the city who's just moved to the island of [Fairhaven](Fairhaven.md) to help out on her [grandmother](Hazel_Wylde.md)'s farm.

Shortly after Tara arrives in Fairhaven, she discovers that she's a witch. Her grandmother is part of a [secret coven](The_Fairhaven_Coven.md) that meets in the woods. So, in addition to her farming duties and social life, Tara must also hone her magical abilities, brew potions and master flying on her broomstick.

### Life Before Fairhaven

Before arriving to Fairhaven, Tara was engaged and had a job. After losing her fiancé and job in the same week, she moves to Fairhaven to care for her grandmother.
