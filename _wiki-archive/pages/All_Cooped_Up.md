**All Cooped Up** is a [quest](To-Do_Quests.md) received after completing the [Give a Ranch Hand](Give_a_Ranch_Hand.md) questline. It requires you to deliver 1000 coins and 100x [Wood](Wood.md) to [Parker Johnson](Parker_Johnson.md) so he can build you a coop. You then buy a chick from [Marty Emerson](Marty_Emerson.md) for 250 coins. The reward for completion is the [Barn](Barn.md) blueprint, the [Butter Churn](Butter_Churn.md) blueprint, and 10x [Chicken Feed](Chicken_Feed.md).
