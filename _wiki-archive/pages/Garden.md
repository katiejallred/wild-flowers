## Garden

Using the fenced-in space next to the [farmhouse](Farm.md), Tara can grow a number of [crops](https://wylde-flowers.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Crops) and can craft areas to produce compost and honey.

## Vegetable Patch

A second garden area located south of the [farmhouse](Farm.md), Tara can grow a number of [crops](https://wylde-flowers.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Crops) and can craft areas to produce compost and honey. [Parker](Parker_Johnson.md) can add this to your farm via [Nailed It!](Nailed_It!.md).
