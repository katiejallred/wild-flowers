Sebastián Miranda (16) is [Violet](Violet_Miranda.md)'s brother. He works in [Sophia Moretti](Sophia_Moretti.md)'s diner in the afternoons. Sebastián seems eager to get off [Fairhaven](Fairhaven.md) and explore the rest of the world.

## Other Information

Sebastián is incredulous that Tara chose to come to Fairhaven from the city, frequently stating that he plans to get out of here the second he's 18. Has the game turn in summer again he finally became a witch as it one year later it mean he is supposed to be seventeen.

**Click to reveal spoilers!**

Sebastián eventually joins the Fairhaven coven.

  
Although his description states that he works in the diner in afternoons, he is never seen there!

Sebastián later confirms that he works part-time at Kai's stall.

Voiced by: Joshua Montes
