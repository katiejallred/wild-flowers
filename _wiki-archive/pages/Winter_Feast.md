# Winter Feast

*Categories: Events*

*Source: <https://wylde-flowers.fandom.com/wiki/Winter_Feast>*

---

**Winter Feast** is a [quest](To-Do_Quests.md) given by [Kim Izumi](Kim_Izumi.md) at the beginning of [Winter](Winter.md) that serves as a 10-day countdown timer to the Winter Feast.

You can receive four free food items during the feast: 1x [Cabbage Soup](Cabbage_Soup.md) from [Amira](Amira_Syed.md), 1x [Beef & Shiitake](Beef_&_Shiitake.md) from [Kim](Kim_Izumi.md), 1x [Sweet Potato Casserole](Sweet_Potato_Casserole.md) from [Angus](Angus_Seton.md), and 1x [Hot Chocolate](Hot_Chocolate.md) from Damon.

Some of the dialogue during the feast changes depending on if the quests [A Fab-Yule-Us Feast](A_Fab-Yule-Us_Feast.md) and [Salmon Has to Do It](Salmon_Has_to_Do_It.md) were completed in time.
