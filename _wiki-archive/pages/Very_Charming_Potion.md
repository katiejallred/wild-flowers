## Description

Drink this potion to become instantly more charismatic to people you interact with. Wears off after 5 days.  
  
Very Charming Potion is crafted at the [Cauldron](Cauldron.md).  
  

  

## Notes

This potion will increase the filling of relationship hearts during interactions.

The very charming potion's effects also apply to [chickens](Chickens.md) and barn animals!
