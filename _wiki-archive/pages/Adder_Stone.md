## Description

A magical, lustrous stone found in the Gloaming. Use it to turn the seasons from Winter to Spring.

## Information

The Adder Stone can be mined from a rock to the East in the second level of the Gloaming. After Year 1, you must cast a [Reverse Time Spell](Reverse_Time_Spell.md) at the spot to bring back the rock and mine it again.
