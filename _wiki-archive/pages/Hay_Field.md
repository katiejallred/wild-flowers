# Hay Field

*Source: <https://wylde-flowers.fandom.com/wiki/Hay_Field>*

---

Once the [Barn](Barn.md) is built, [Parker](Parker_Johnson.md) can build the Hay Field, allowing all barn animals to graze. This means Tara is no longer required to buy [Hay](Hay.md) from [Marty](Marty_Emerson.md) or feed them.

The cost is 2,000 coins and 40 soil.
