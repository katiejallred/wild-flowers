# Crafting

*Categories: Game Mechanics*

*Source: <https://wylde-flowers.fandom.com/wiki/Crafting>*

---

Crafting is anything that Tara creates on her farm, using foraged, grown, or purchased items. There are 3 crafting locations on the farm: the [tool shed](Tool_Shed.md), the [basement](Basement.md), and the [garden](Garden.md). You must first craft machinery to then craft items.

## [Tool Shed](Tool_Shed.md)

The tool shed is a space next to Tara's farmhouse where she can build a variety of crafting stations to refine resources. Originally (**size**), it can be upgraded by [Parker Johnson](Parker_Johnson.md) to have a usable layout of 7x12 (total size 9x12). The upgrade costs 50x [wood](Wood.md) and (**amount**) coins and requires Tara to clear the surrounding space, which may call for a wood axe and/or pickaxe. The upgrade will be complete on the second morning after purchasing.

  

![Paper Press](../images/Paper_Press.png)

### [Paper Press](Paper_Press.md)

A device that presses wood into [paper](Paper.md).

  

![Loom](../images/Loom.png)

### [Loom](Loom.md)

Used to make [cloth](Cloth.md) from cotton and wool or [silk](Silk.md) from silk thread.

  

![Candle Maker](../images/Candle_Maker.png)

### [Candlemaker](Candlemaker.md)

A device for making candles out of beeswax, soy milk, or animal fat.

  

![Juice Press](../images/Juice_Press.png)

### [Juice Press](Juice_Press.md)

A device that extracts juices and oils from raw fruits and vegetables.

  

![Dyeing Bath](../images/Dyeing_Bath.png)

### [Dyeing Bath](Dyeing_Bath.md)

A large bucket for dyeing fabric. Make dyes with the mortar and pestle.

  

![Metalworking Table](../images/Metalworking_Table.png)

### [Metalworking Table](Metalworking_Table.md)

A workstation for creating metal jewelry.

  

![Fermentation Barrel](../images/Fermentation_Barrel.png)

### [Fermentation Barrel](Fermentation_Barrel.md)

A big wooden barrel used to make [beer](Beer.md), [wine](Wine.md), [cheese](Cheese.md), and other interesting recipes!

  

![Glass Kiln](../images/Glass_Kiln.png)

### [Glass Kiln](Glass_Kiln.md)

A furnace powered by [coal](Coal.md). It turns high-quality sand into [glass](Glass.md).

  

![Silkworm Box](../images/Silkworm_Box.png)

### [Silkworm Box](Silkworm_Box.md)

A silkworm house. Fill it with silkworms and feed them [mulberry leaves](Mulberry_Leaf.md) to get [silk thread](Silk_Thread.md).

  

![Butter Churn](../images/Butter_Churn.png)

### [Butter Churn](Butter_Churn.md)

A device that makes [butter](Butter.md) from [milk](Milk.md) or milk alternatives.

  

## [Basement](Basement.md)

The basement is where Tara will craft magic items. Originally outfitted with a [cauldron](Cauldron.md), you will have to add other machinery and furniture in order to craft all magic items. The basement has 9x10 useable spaces.

![Cauldron](../images/Cauldron.JPEG)

### [Cauldron](Cauldron.md)

The cauldron is included with the introduction of the basement. Used to craft potions.

  

![Distillery](../images/Distillery.JPEG)

### [Distillery](Distillery.md)

The distillery is used to craft ingredients for potions, drinks, and liquor.

  

![Desk](../images/Desk.JPEG)

### [Desk](Desk.md)

The desk is for crafting incantations and ink.

  

![Crystal Ball](../images/Crystal_Ball.JPEG)

### [Crystal Ball](Crystal_Ball.md)

The crystal ball can see the "desires" (read: [favorite foods](Gifting.md)) of any character, using a [crystal ball potion](Crystal_Ball_Potion.md).

  

![Scrying Bowl](../images/Scrying_Bowl.JPEG)

### [Scrying Bowl](Scrying_Bowl.md)

The scrying bowl can find anything you have misplaced, using the [scrying bowl potion](Scrying_Potion.md).

  

![Mortar and Pestle](../images/Mortar_and_Pestle.JPEG)

### Mortar and Pestle

The mortar and pestle is used to create dyes for fabric, crushed powders, and [flour](Flour.md).

  

## [Garden](Garden.md)

The garden is where Tara grows fruit trees, vegetables, and flowers. Hazel's home starts with an 8x8 garden beside it, but Tara can purchase an 11x12 vegetable garden from Parker.

![Garden Bed](../images/Garden_Bed.jpg)

### [Garden Bed](Garden_Bed.md)

The garden bed is used for planting 4 seeds or one tree.

  

### [Compost Bin](Compost_Bin.md)

The compost bin creates [soil](Soil.md) using two weeds from the farm.

### [Beehive](Bee_Hive.md)

The beehive creates honey to be sold or crafted with.
