## Ravenwood Hollow

Ravenwood Hollow is a location near Fairhaven.

| Ravenwood Hollow is a Fae town that can be reached by repairing the boat that will appear on the small lake by Hazel's farm. It is home to [Zephyr Grimspark,](Zephyr_Grimspark.md) [Peri,](Peri.md) [Aryel](Aryel.md) and [Westley Vuk.](Westley_Vuk.md) [Aryel](Aryel.md) runs [Pig's Wings and Dragonlings](Pig's_Wings_and_Dragonlings.md) (on the left of the landing place) a shop for animal care. Peri runs [Peri's Apothecary](Peri's_Apothecary.md) (on the right), where you can buy magical ingredients for portioning and incantations. [Zephyr Grimspark](Zephyr_Grimspark.md) owns [Magical Mechanicals](Magical_Mechanicals.md) (on the right), a shop where you can have your broom calibrated and buy magical machines to help you on your farm. [Westley Vuk](Westley_Vuk.md) runs [Arcane Texts](Arcane_Texts.md), a magical bookshop. There is also a notice board, similar to the one in town, where you can fulfill orders for the inhabitants of Ravenwood Hollow. [Shelby](Shelby_Demeter.md) can sometimes be seen visiting the town. |
