## Description

A creamy sheet of paper made from pressed wood. Use it to write on.

## Other information

Paper is used to craft most incantations, making it one of the most important resources in the game

## Quests

Paper is used in the following [quests](Quests.md):

- [Incantation Vexation](Quests.md)
