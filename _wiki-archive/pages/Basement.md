## Basement

The basement is a space in Tara's farmhouse where she can build a variety of crafting stations to create [magical items](Magic_Items.md). This workspace is critical to Tara's progression through the ranks of [The Fairhaven Coven](The_Fairhaven_Coven.md) as many of its quests require items crafted here.

  

![Cauldron](../images/Cauldron.JPEG)

### [Cauldron](Cauldron.md)

A witch's trusty vessel for brewing potions. Build more cauldrons to keep a steady supply of potions at your fingertips! (Note: The basement comes equipped with one cauldron and more cannot be built until the ability is unlocked later in the game)

![Desk](../images/Desk.JPEG)

### [Desk](Desk.md)

Your incantation station. Use it to make ink as well.

  

![Distillery](../images/Distillery.JPEG)

### [Distillery](Distillery.md)

A device that extracts essences and distillations. Required for many potions and some drinks.

  

![Mortar and Pestle](../images/Mortar_and_Pestle.JPEG)

### [Mortar and Pestle](Mortar_and_Pestle.md)

A device for crushing raw ingredients. Use it to make dyes and powders.

  

![Scrying Bowl](../images/Scrying_Bowl.JPEG)

### [Scrying Bowl](Scrying_Bowl.md)

An enchanted bowl. Fill it with [Scrying Potion](Scrying_Potion.md) to find the location of people or objects.

  

![Crystal Ball](../images/Crystal_Ball.JPEG)

### [Crystal Ball](Crystal_Ball.md)

A magical device that reveals innermost desires. Requires [Crystal Ball Solution](Crystal_Ball_Solution.md), brewed in a [Cauldron](Cauldron.md).
