**Natalia Kuznetsova** (36) is the town [Blacksmith](Blacksmith.md) so she can upgrade [tools](Tools.md) and smelt raw ore. She's strong, capable, and cheerful, but thinks she has been cursed to suffer eternal bad luck. Her happiness in [Fairhaven](Fairhaven.md) has made her wonder if the curse has lifted.

Recently, Natalia joined the [League of the Conscious Mind](League_of_the_Conscious_Mind.md) due to a fondness for [Cameron Conner](Cameron_Conner.md).

## Life Before Fairhaven

Before moving to Fairhaven, she was an insurance claims adjustor with no blacksmithing experience. She has a family history of blacksmithing, however - her hammer belonged to her great-grandfather.
