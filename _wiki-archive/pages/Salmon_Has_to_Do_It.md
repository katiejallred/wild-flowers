# Salmon Has to Do It

*Categories: Quests*

*Source: <https://wylde-flowers.fandom.com/wiki/Salmon_Has_to_Do_It>*

---

**Salmon Has to Do It** is a [quest](To-Do_Quests.md) given by [Kim Izumi](Kim_Izumi.md) at the beginning of [Winter](Winter.md). It requires the player to cook 1x [Roast Salmon](Roast_Salmon.md) as your household's contribution to the [Winter Feast](Winter_Feast.md). The meal must be in your inventory the morning of the Feast to complete the quest.
