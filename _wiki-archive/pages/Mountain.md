# Mountain

*Categories: Areas*

*Source: <https://wylde-flowers.fandom.com/wiki/Mountain>*

---

![Mountain1](../images/Mountain1.png) ![Mountain2](../images/Mountain2.png) ![Mountain3](../images/Mountain3.png)

The **Mountain** area is initially inaccessible until the connecting bridge is upgraded during the [Summer](Summer.md) season. It hosts a variety of forgeable goods, including respawning trees that can be chopped for [wood](Wood.md). It also has the unique feature of periodically-spawning dig spots that can reveal a small amount of coins or a new [Cooking](Cooking.md) recipe!

## Broken Bridge

There's a note when you select the bridge before it's fixed.  
**Danger!** This bridge has been declared unfit for use. The path to the mountain is off-limits. Parker

## Connections

- [Farm](Farm.md) (bottom left; exits to northwest)

## Residents

- [Giva Joshi](Giva_Joshi.md)

## Businesses

There are no traditional businesses in the Mountain area, but [Giva](Giva.md) can be found during work hours in her Weather Tower (up at the top of the mountain to the east) and, after completing a quest for [Kim](Kim.md), Tara can visit the **Onsen** in the lower left of the area once a day to restore a portion of her energy -- for a price!
