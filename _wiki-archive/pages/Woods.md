# Woods

*Categories: Areas*

*Source: <https://wylde-flowers.fandom.com/wiki/Woods>*

---

![Forest1](../images/Forest1.png) ![Forest2](../images/Forest2.png) ![ForestWinter(2)](../images/ForestWinter%282%29.png)

**The Woods** is a wooded area to the far east of Fairhaven where many resources can be foraged, including respawning trees for collecting [wood](Wood.md). There are opportunities for fishing, and its upper left area serves as the meeting place for the [Fairhaven Coven](Fairhaven_Coven.md)!

**First time interacting with the Silver Willow Tree**  
**Tara:** Hmmm, there's a plaque that reads: *This beloved tree be olde as thee towne itselfe*

**First time interacting with the Bramble Gate**  
**Tara:** There's whispering...what is that? Something spooky and weird is going on here.
