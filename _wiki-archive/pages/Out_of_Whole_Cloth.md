**Out of Whole Cloth** is a [quest](To-Do_Quests.md) given by [Thomas Lightfoot](Thomas_Lightfoot.md) at the beginning of [Summer](Summer.md). It requires you to grow 4x [Cotton](Cotton.md), build a [Loom](Loom.md), and weave the cotton into [Cloth](Cloth.md). The reward for completion are the [Red Dye](Red_Dye.md), [Blue Dye](Blue_Dye.md), and [Purple Dye](Purple_Dye.md) recipes and the [Dyeing Bath](Dyeing_Bath.md) blueprint.

Thomas says he sells his cloth to the [General Store](Dahl_General_Store.md), but Tara can only sell cloth to [Kai's stall](Kai's_Treasures.md).

I guess you can have a couple of my dyes, too. (Gives 2 red dye, 2 blue dye)
