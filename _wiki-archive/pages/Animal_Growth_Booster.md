## Description

"Speeds up the growth stages for animals."  
  
Animal Growth Booster is crafted at the Desk.  
  

  
This incantation was not originally a part of the game, and was added as a part of the Fabulous Farming update that was released on April 27th, 2023. This update included new animals, the opportunity for animals to get pregnant/produce babies, a larger barn and coop, and magical animals.
