**Oh, the Iron-y** is a [quest](To-Do_Quests.md) given by [Natalia Kuznetsova](Natalia_Kuznetsova.md) after the mine entrance is repaired. It requires you to deliver 3x [Iron Ore](Iron_Ore.md) to her. The reward for completion is 3x [Iron Ingots](Iron_Ingot.md) and 80 coins.

Dialogue

Quest intro:

- Natalia: Whohoo!!!!! It’s looking great! D’you know what that means?
- Tara: I need builder’s insurance?
- Natalia: Hahaha, silly! You can go mining!
- Tara: Yeah, it was kind of implied.
- Natalia: Here, you’ll need this to get started. \[Gives rusty pickaxe.\] A pickaxe is a handy tool to have around. You can break up rocks to gather some stone and also mine lots of lovely ore now this place is fixed up! Now, take it in and get cracking!
- Tara: But… it’s dark and there’s probably bugs…
- Natalia: You’ll want to focus on cracking open the mineral rocks to find ore. Bring me back some iron ore and I can turn it into iron for you! Before I forget, you’ll need to keep your eyes peeled for the keys to get farther into the mine. Some clever fellow has scattered them about. They’re probably buried under falling rocks by now. But there’s lots of great stuff in the mine, so it’s worth it! Have fun exploring!
- Tara: Oh, I’m sure I will. Yay.

Quest completion:

- Natalia: Ohmygosh, is that iron ore?
- Tara: Um, I think this dirty rock might be some?
- Natalia: It sure is! Look at that, you’re a natural! This is great! Save some time and take these iron ingots I made. \[Gives 3x Iron Ingot.\] I’ll melt these down. I can swap your iron ore for iron anytime, but I’ll charge a little for the work.
- \[Reward: 80 coins\]
