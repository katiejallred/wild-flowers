# Working notes for this repo

Read this before writing any prose that people will read: Reddit posts and comments,
site copy, PR descriptions, email. The site's reference briefing is in
`_briefing/cowork-briefing.md`. Reddit drafts are not kept in this repo; hand them
back in chat.

## Don't write like an AI caption

A reader on Reddit flagged these as the tells that make a post read like a Meta AI
Instagram caption. Avoid every one of them, in Katie's voice and in the site's voice.

**Banned patterns**

- **Contrast couplets as punchlines.** Two short parallel sentences that pit one thing
  against another: "Midnight is a nudge. 2 AM is a wall." / "A picked flower is a
  one-time coin. A blooming flower is an income." / "None of them are spoilers. All of
  them would have saved me a season." Also "It's not X, it's Y" and "This isn't about
  X. It's about Y." If you want a contrast, make it one full sentence with the reason
  in it: "If you push past 2 AM, Tara collapses and wakes up with a smaller meter."
- **The "things nobody told you" frame.** No "N things the game never tells you,"
  "nobody told me," "what I wish I'd known," "the tutorial won't tell you this." Say
  what the post actually is: five rules, a gift table, a harvest checklist.
- **Aphorism closers.** No tidy one-liners that sum up a paragraph: "That's the whole
  trick." / "Unspent energy is a harvest you left in the field." / "Pack every box,
  then light the candles." End on the fact or the instruction instead.
- **Rhythm fragments.** No runs of three short fragments for effect ("Festivals wait.
  Story scenes wait. Plant anything."). One short sentence per paragraph at most, and
  only when it carries information.
- **Rhetorical setups.** No "Here's the thing." / "So let's settle it." / "You can
  breathe." / "Be honest:" as a lead-in. Start with the point.

**The test:** if a sentence would work on its own as an Instagram caption or a
motivational slide, rewrite it as something only this page would say, usually by
adding the specific number, name, or reason.

**Site asides.** The journal layout's handwritten asides (`<span class="handwritten">`),
"sign-off tips," and "lore asides" are the places this creeps in most. An aside earns
its spot only if it carries a concrete tip, a specific detail, or a plain in-fiction
observation ("Hazel's tea smells of herbs I cannot name"). An aside that is only a
slogan ("a first season is for roots, not riches") gets rewritten into the tip it was
gesturing at, or cut.

**What to do instead:** plain sentences with a verb and a specific. Put the number in
("about 12 blooming flowers per hive"), the name in ("Kai sells seedlings for 125"),
or the consequence in ("out-of-season crops vanish when the season turns"). Katie's
voice still applies for anything posted under her name (load the
`katie-allred-voice` skill), but her "contrast pairs" move is off the table for
Reddit, since that is the exact pattern readers now read as AI.

## Other standing rules

- Reddit posts and comments: Katie's voice, never the site's journal voice. Answer in
  the comment, link the Guide second, say it's Katie's own site. Full playbook in
  `_briefing/cowork-briefing.md`.
- Game facts come from the Guide's own pages, not memory. If a thread corrects a fact,
  fix the page too.

  characters.
